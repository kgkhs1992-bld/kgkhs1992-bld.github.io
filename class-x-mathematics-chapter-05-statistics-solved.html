🧮 Solved Examples

ଉଦାହରଣ 1 – Mean

2,4,6,8,10 ର mean।

Mean=30/5=6.

ଉଦାହରଣ 2 – Median

3,7,5,9,1 ର median।

Order: 1,3,5,7,9. Median=5.

ଉଦାହରଣ 3 – Even Median

2,4,6,8 ର median।

(4+6)/2=5.

ଉଦାହରଣ 4 – Mode

2,3,3,4,5,3,6 ର mode।

Mode=3.

ଉଦାହରଣ 5 – Mean property

M mean ହେଲେ Σ(xᵢ−M)=?

Σ(xᵢ−M)=0.
