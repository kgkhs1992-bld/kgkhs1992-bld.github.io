⭐ Additional Questions & Answers

Extra practice questions prepared from the chapter content.

Q1. Why is the unknown language important to the poem?
Answer: It makes the song mysterious while allowing the melody itself to communicate emotion.
Q2. How does the poet react to the girl’s work?
Answer: He stops to observe and listen to her singing.
Q3. What contrast is created between the poet and the girl?
Answer: The girl works and sings naturally, while the poet observes and imagines the meaning of her song.
Q4. Why does the poet use comparisons with birds?
Answer: The comparisons help express the beauty and emotional power of the song.
Q5. How does nature support the mood?
Answer: The Highland landscape provides a quiet setting that makes the solitary song more striking.
Q6. What makes the song memorable?
Answer: Its melody and emotional effect remain with the poet even after he leaves.
Q7. What is the significance of the word “solitary”?
Answer: It emphasizes the girl’s isolation and the individuality of her singing.
Q8. What is the poet’s main limitation as a listener?
Answer: He cannot understand the words.
Q9. Does the poet still appreciate the song without understanding its words?
Answer: Yes. Its sound and emotional effect are enough to move him.
Q10. What broad idea does the poem suggest about music?
Answer: Music can communicate feeling even when its words are not understood.
