Book Q & A / Text Understanding

1. Who wrote Home and Love?
Answer: Robert William Service.
2. What are the two key words in the poem?
Answer: Home and Love.
3. How does the poet describe the words?
Answer: They are small words but carry deep and tender meaning.
4. What does the poet say about home without love?
Answer: He presents it as bitter.
5. What does he say about love without home?
Answer: He presents it as painful.
6. Can Home and Love be separated in the poem's idea?
Answer: The poet says they naturally belong together.
7. What does 'hand and glove' suggest?
Answer: It suggests being closely connected.
8. What does the poet say about having both home and love?
Answer: A person who has both has reason for great happiness.
9. Does the poem say wealth is necessary for happiness?
Answer: No. It suggests home and love can make life joyful even when a person is poor.
10. Who are said to use sweet words in Heaven?
Answer: Angels.
11. What does 'gracious' mean?
Answer: Pleasant, generous or kindly in the context of the poem.
12. What does 'tenderly' mean?
Answer: With gentle and caring feeling.
13. What does 'bitterness' mean?
Answer: A feeling of anger, resentment or emotional pain.
14. What does 'seldom' mean?
Answer: Hardly ever or rarely.
15. What does 'divine' mean?
Answer: Associated with God or something supremely wonderful.
16. What is the main theme?
Answer: The emotional value of a loving home.
17. Why does the poet repeat Home and Love?
Answer: The repetition emphasizes their importance and close relationship.
18. What message does the poem give families?
Answer: Value the love and warmth found in a home.
19. What does the poem suggest about material poverty?
Answer: It suggests that love and home can bring happiness even without wealth.
20. What is the tone of the poem?
Answer: Warm, affectionate and appreciative.
