📐 Chapter 04 – Probability

🔄 Quick Revision

ମୁଖ୍ୟ ସୂତ୍ର

Empirical: P(E)=ଘଟଣା ଘଟିଥିବା ଥର ÷ ମୋଟ ପରୀକ୍ଷଣ

Theoretical: P(E)=Favourable outcomes ÷ Total equally likely outcomes

Complement: P(E̅)=1−P(E)

Sample Space

1 coin → {H,T}; 2 coins → {HH,HT,TH,TT}; 1 die → {1,2,3,4,5,6}; 2 dice → 36 ordered outcomes.

Events

  • Event = Sample Space ର ଉପସମୁଚ୍ଚୟ
  • Simple Event = ଗୋଟିଏ ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ outcome
  • Impossible event → P=0
  • Sure event → P=1

Exam Steps

  1. Experiment ଚିହ୍ନଟ କର।
  2. Sample Space ଲେଖ।
  3. Event ଚିହ୍ନଟ କର।
  4. Favourable outcomes ଗଣ।
  5. Total outcomes ଗଣ।
  6. Formula ବ୍ୟବହାର କର।
  7. 0≤P≤1 ଦ୍ୱାରା ଯାଞ୍ଚ କର।
