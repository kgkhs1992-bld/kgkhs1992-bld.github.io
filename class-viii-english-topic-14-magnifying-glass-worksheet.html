F. Short answers – key points

  • The spider shows fascinating details of web-making and body structure.
  • The moon extends the lens idea to distant celestial objects.
  • Magnifying spectacles, stand magnifiers, hand-held magnifiers and telescopes.
  • /v/ has vibration; /f/ does not.
  • Aryabhata, Varahamihira and Bhaskaracharya II.
