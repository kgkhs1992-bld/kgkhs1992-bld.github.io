OUR DIGITAL JOURNEY

How Our School Website Was Built

🌱 A Journey of Learning

The KGKHS Beldandia website is more than just a website. It represents the learning, effort, patience and dedication behind bringing our school into the digital world.

💻 From an Idea to a Website

The journey began with a simple idea — to create a digital platform for Karua Gadadhar Kar High School, Beldandia, where students, parents, teachers and visitors could learn about our school and its activities.

📚 Learning Through Every Step

Building the website involved learning HTML, CSS, JavaScript, file management, PDF organisation, student results and many other digital skills.

🛠️ Testing, Mistakes and Improvements

The journey was not always easy. There were broken pages, missing files, 404 errors, design problems and many things that had to be corrected. Every mistake became another opportunity to learn.

🎓 A Digital Learning Platform

Today, the website is developing into a learning platform containing textbooks, study materials, practice questions, MCQs, student results, school information, notices and educational resources.

🤝 A Journey of Dedication

Every page, every correction and every improvement reflects the belief that technology can support education and give students better access to learning resources.

❤️ Our Message

This digital journey reminds us that progress does not happen in one day. It happens step by step — through learning, testing, correcting and never giving up.

ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Karua Gadadhar Kar High School

Student Facilities

Karua Gadadhar Kar High School was established in 1992 and is managed by the Department of Education. The school is located in a rural area at Karua Grampanchayat, Basta Block, Balasore District, Odisha.

The school consists of Grades 8 to 10 and is co-educational. It does not have an attached pre-primary section. The medium of instruction is Odia. The school is approachable by an all-weather road and the academic session begins in April.

Our Inspiration and History

The legendary person Late Kanhu Kishore Kar was the first donor to the school. Another local personality, Mr. Niranjan Patra from Palsahi village, has been a backbone of the school and has contributed greatly to its development.

The real hero of the school, Mr. Abhiram Kamila, spent his entire professional life working for the upliftment and development of the school. He later became the Headmaster and served the institution with dedication. He retired from service on 31 May 2026, handing over charge to Mr. Trilochan Panda, the 3rd TGT of the school. Mr. Trilochan Panda is now serving as the IC Headmaster and continues his efforts to make the school one of the best educational institutions in the Balasore district.

School Campus and Facilities

The school has a private building situated on approximately 60 decimals of land and has around 3 acres of its own land. It has 3 classrooms, one computer laboratory and one science laboratory for instructional purposes. All classrooms are in good condition. The school also has two additional rooms for non-teaching activities and a separate room for the Headmaster/teachers.

The school has had a hostel facility for its students from the very beginning, and the hostel facility is provided free of cost. The school has a boundary wall and electricity connection.

The school has functional drinking-water facilities, including tap water and machine water providing chilled, hot and normal water. There are 4 boys' toilets and 3 girls' toilets, all functional.

The school has a playground and a library with 1,352 books. It also has computers for teaching and learning purposes. Under the 5T programme of the Government of Odisha, the school has 5 computer-aided learning facilities.

Welcome to Karua Gadadhar Kar High School, Beldandia!

As the Headmaster of this esteemed institution, it is both a privilege and a profound responsibility to lead a community dedicated to academic excellence, character building, and holistic student development.

Warm regards,
Headmaster

ବେଲଦଣ୍ଡିଆର ଏହି ମାଟିରେ ଆମର ଏହି ବିଦ୍ୟାଳୟ ଦୀର୍ଘ ଦିନ ହେବ ଶିକ୍ଷାର ଏକ ଆଲୋକବର୍ତ୍ତିକା ସାଜି, ଯୁବ ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କଗୁଡ଼ିକୁ ପାଳନ ପୋଷଣ କରିଆସୁଛି ଏବଂ ସେମାନଙ୍କୁ ଏକ ପରିବର୍ତ୍ତନଶୀଳ ଦୁନିଆରେ ଆଗକୁ ବଢ଼ିବା ପାଇଁ ଆବଶ୍ୟକ ଜ୍ଞାନ, ମୂଲ୍ୟବୋଧ ଓ ଦକ୍ଷତା ପ୍ରଦାନ କରୁଛି। ଶିକ୍ଷା କେବଳ ପାଠ୍ୟପୁସ୍ତକ ମଧ୍ୟରେ ସୀମିତ ନୁହେଁ, ବରଂ ଏହା ଆତ୍ମ-ଆବିଷ୍କାର, ଜିଜ୍ଞାସା ଏବଂ ଦୃଢ଼ତାର ଏକ ଯାତ୍ରା ବୋଲି ଆମେ ଦୃଢ଼ ଭାବରେ ବିଶ୍ୱାସ କରୁ।

• ସର୍ବାଙ୍ଗୀନ ବିକାଶ: ଆମେ ଏକ ଏପରି ପରିବେଶ ସୃଷ୍ଟି କରୁଛୁ ଯାହା ଶିକ୍ଷାଗତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମ ସହିତ ସହ-ପାଠ୍ୟକ୍ରମ, କ୍ରୀଡ଼ା, କଳା ଏବଂ ସମାଜସେବାକୁ ସନ୍ତୁଳିତ କରିଥାଏ।

• ନିଷ୍ଠାପର ଶିକ୍ଷକ ମଣ୍ଡଳୀ: ଆମର ଅଭିଜ୍ଞ ଏବଂ ଉତ୍ସର୍ଗୀକୃତ ଶିକ୍ଷକମାନେ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ଛାତ୍ରଛାତ୍ରୀଙ୍କୁ ମାର୍ଗଦର୍ଶନ କରି ସେମାନଙ୍କର ଅନନ୍ୟ ପ୍ରତିଭାକୁ ବିକଶିତ କରିବାରେ ସହାୟତା କରନ୍ତି।

• ମୂଲ୍ୟବୋଧ ଭିତ୍ତିକ ସଂସ୍କୃତି: ସଚ୍ଚୋଟତା, ସହାନୁଭୂତି, ଶୃଙ୍ଖଳା ଏବଂ ପାରସ୍ପରିକ ଆଦର ଆମର ଦୈନନ୍ଦିନ ବିଦ୍ୟାଳୟ ଜୀବନର ମୂଳଦୁଆ ଅଟେ, ଯାହା ଆମର ଛାତ୍ରଛାତ୍ରୀମାନଙ୍କୁ ଦାୟିତ୍ୱବାନ ଏବଂ ସମବେଦନାଶୀଳ ନାଗରିକ ଭାବରେ ଗଢ଼ିତୋଳେ।

ଆମର ପ୍ରିୟ ଛାତ୍ରଛାତ୍ରୀମାନେ: ମନେରଖନ୍ତୁ ଯେ ଶିକ୍ଷାଲାଭ ଏକ କ୍ରମାଗତ ଅଭିଯାନ ଅଟେ। ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ଆହ୍ୱାନକୁ ଆଗକୁ ବଢ଼ିବାର ଏକ ସୁଯୋଗ ଭାବରେ ଗ୍ରହଣ କରନ୍ତୁ, ଜିଜ୍ଞାସୁ ରୁହନ୍ତୁ ଏବଂ କେବେବି ବଡ଼ ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ରଖିବାରୁ ପଛଘୁଞ୍ଚା ଦିଅନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ।
