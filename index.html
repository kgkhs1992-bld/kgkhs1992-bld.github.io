ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Learning, character and opportunity.

Karua Gadadhar Kar High School, Beldandia was established on 20 July 1992. The school provides secondary education for students from Class 8th through Class 10th.

Our medium of learning is Odia and English. This website provides a central place for students, parents and the community to find school information and announcements.
