The Magic Flute

1-Mark Questions & Answers • 30 Questions
← Back to Class IX English
Q1. Who is the main boy in the story?
Answer: Sukumar.
Q2. How old was Sukumar?
Answer: About eleven years old.
Q3. Where did Sukumar arrive?
Answer: At a small village.
Q4. What mountains were visible from the village?
Answer: The Himalayas.
Q5. Why did Sukumar play the flute when he arrived?
Answer: He was reminded of his dead mother.
Q6. Who was the middle-aged woman Sukumar met?
Answer: The village headman's wife.
Q7. What did the woman call Sukumar?
Answer: Her son.
Q8. Why did the woman take Sukumar home?
Answer: She felt a bond with him and wanted him to stay.
Q9. When did Sukumar lose his mother?
Answer: At the age of five.
Q10. What did Sukumar believe about his mother?
Answer: He believed she still lived in his heart.
Q11. Who gave Sukumar his flute?
Answer: His father.
Q12. Why did Sukumar want a flute?
Answer: He wanted to feel close to his mother by playing it.
Q13. Who taught Sukumar to play the flute?
Answer: His father.
Q14. What happened to Sukumar's father?
Answer: He died.
Q15. Who was Sayapatri?
Answer: The village headman's daughter.
Q16. How did Sayapatri and Sukumar become related?
Answer: They became brother and sister in the family.
Q17. Why did Sukumar feel sad at the headman's house?
Answer: The headman did not accept him warmly.
Q18. How did the family react to Sukumar's flute playing?
Answer: They were deeply moved by his music.
Q19. Why did Sukumar decide to leave the house?
Answer: He could not win the headman's love.
Q20. Where did Sukumar take shelter?
Answer: In a shrine on a mountain.
Q21. How did Sukumar feel in the shrine?
Answer: Lonely and sad.
Q22. Whom did Sukumar dream about?
Answer: Sayapatri.
Q23. Why did Sukumar return to the house?
Answer: He dreamed of Sayapatri and felt he should return.
Q24. What had happened to Sayapatri?
Answer: She had a very high fever and was in a coma.
Q25. What did Sayapatri's mother ask Sukumar to do?
Answer: She asked him to revive Sayapatri.
Q26. What did Sukumar play?
Answer: His flute.
Q27. What happened when Sukumar played the flute?
Answer: Sayapatri's eyelids fluttered and she opened her eyes.
Q28. What did the headman finally call Sukumar?
Answer: His dear son.
Q29. What did the headman do to Sukumar?
Answer: He hugged him.
Q30. Why is the flute called magical?
Answer: Its music helped bring Sayapatri back to consciousness and reunited the family.
