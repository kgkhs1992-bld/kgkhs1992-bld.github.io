Chapter 03 – Road Safety Week

Additional Questions • Class IX English • Jasmine

Additional Questions

One-mark practice questions.

  1. State one reason road safety awareness is necessary.
    Answer: Road safety awareness.
  2. Name the national road-safety awareness programme discussed in the chapter.
    Answer: Road Safety Week.
  3. Which month is mentioned for Road Safety Week?
    Answer: January.
  4. Name the ministry mentioned as organising the programme.
    Answer: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways.
  5. What is the danger of driving after drinking alcohol?
    Answer: It can cause rash driving and serious accidents.
  6. What is meant by high-speed driving?
    Answer: Driving faster than is safe or permitted.
  7. Why should drivers avoid texting?
    Answer: It distracts the driver.
  8. What is a pedestrian?
    Answer: A person travelling on foot.
  9. What is a commuter?
    Answer: A regular traveller.
  10. What does 'paramount' mean?
    Answer: More important than anything else.
  11. What does 'prevent' mean?
    Answer: To check or put an end to something.
  12. What does 'logistic' mean?
    Answer: Support needed to run an activity or campaign.
  13. What does 'campaign' mean?
    Answer: A planned series of activities for a purpose.
  14. What does 'voluntarily' mean?
    Answer: Willingly or freely.
  15. What does 'fatality' mean?
    Answer: Death or loss caused by an accident.
  16. What does 'distracted' mean?
    Answer: Having attention disturbed or diverted.
  17. What does 'precious' mean?
    Answer: Very valuable.
  18. Name one activity organised during Road Safety Week.
    Answer: Posters, films, debates or workshops.
  19. Name one safety item used by a biker.
    Answer: A helmet.
  20. Name one safety item used by a car occupant.
    Answer: A seat belt.
  21. Why are speed limits necessary?
    Answer: They reduce dangerous speeds.
  22. Why should vehicles be checked for fitness?
    Answer: To check whether vehicles are safe for use.
  23. What is one cause of distracted driving?
    Answer: Phone use or texting while driving.
  24. What is one consequence of ignoring traffic signals?
    Answer: It can cause collisions and injuries.
  25. Why should unqualified persons not drive?
    Answer: They may not be legally qualified to drive.
  26. What is the role of road signs?
    Answer: They provide instructions and warnings.
  27. How can schools help road safety?
    Answer: By joining awareness programmes.
  28. Why are medical check-up camps useful for drivers?
    Answer: They help assess drivers' health.
  29. Why should pedestrians remain alert?
    Answer: They should stay alert and use safe crossings.
  30. State the main message of Road Safety Week.
    Answer: Protect life and reduce accidents.
