Chapter 07 – Nine Gold Medals

Book Q & A • Class IX English • Jasmine

Book Q & A / Text Understanding

1. Who wrote Nine Gold Medals?
Answer: David Roth.
2. How many athletes were in the race?
Answer: Nine.
3. Where had the athletes come from?
Answer: They had come from different parts of the country.
4. Why had they trained?
Answer: They had trained for the athletic event and hoped to compete for medals.
5. What was the event?
Answer: A hundred-yard dash.
6. What happened at the start?
Answer: The runners charged forward after the starting signal.
7. What happened to the smallest runner?
Answer: He stumbled and fell to the ground.
8. How did he feel after falling?
Answer: He felt frustrated and anguished because his hopes seemed to be lost.
9. What did the other eight runners do?
Answer: They stopped and went back to help him.
10. How did they help him?
Answer: They brought him to his feet and then continued with him.
11. How did the race change?
Answer: The runners stopped competing individually and walked together.
12. What did the nine runners do with their hands?
Answer: They joined hands.
13. What did the banner say?
Answer: Special Olympics.
14. How did the race end?
Answer: All nine crossed the finish line together.
15. Why were there nine gold medals?
Answer: The spirit of cooperation and mutual help made all nine worthy of the same honour.
16. What is a standing ovation?
Answer: Applause in which people stand to show strong appreciation.
17. What did the nine faces show?
Answer: They showed happiness and satisfaction.
18. What is the main message?
Answer: Humanity and helping others can be more valuable than winning alone.
19. What quality did the runners show?
Answer: Empathy and fellow feeling.
20. What does the poem teach about sportsmanship?
Answer: True sportsmanship includes respect, cooperation and helping a fellow competitor.
