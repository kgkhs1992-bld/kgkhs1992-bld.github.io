⭐ Additional Questions & Answers

Extra practice questions prepared from the chapter content.

Q1. Why does the poem include both great and small creatures?
Answer: It shows the variety of creation, from large creatures to tiny living things.
Q2. How does the poem connect nature with gratitude?
Answer: After describing natural beauty, the poet expresses gratitude to the Creator.
Q3. What is the importance of the five senses in the poem?
Answer: They allow people to see, hear, taste, touch and smell the world around them.
Q4. Why are flowers and birds presented together?
Answer: They represent different forms of beauty—colour in flowers and song and movement in birds.
Q5. What does the repeated idea of creation emphasize?
Answer: It emphasizes that the different parts of nature belong to one ordered creation.
Q6. What is the central idea of the poem?
Answer: The central idea is appreciation of the beauty and variety of creation.
Q7. What kind of poem is it in spirit?
Answer: It is both a description of nature and an expression of praise and gratitude.
Q8. Name four natural objects described in the poem.
Answer: Mountains, rivers, flowers and trees are four examples.
Q9. What does the final stanza contribute?
Answer: It connects human senses with the ability to recognize and praise creation.
Q10. What lesson can a student take from the poem?
Answer: A student can learn to observe nature carefully and appreciate its beauty.
