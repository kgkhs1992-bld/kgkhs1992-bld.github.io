CLASS X MATHEMATICS

CHAPTER 01 — LINEAR SIMULTANEOUS EQUATIONS

📖 1. Study Material

A linear equation in two variables has the form ax + by + c = 0. A pair of linear equations in two variables is studied together to find their common solution.

a₁x + b₁y + c₁ = 0
a₂x + b₂y + c₂ = 0

Geometrical representation

  • Intersecting lines → one solution.
  • Parallel distinct lines → no solution.
  • Coincident lines → infinitely many solutions.

Conditions for solvability

a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂ → unique solution
a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ → no solution
a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂ → infinitely many solutions

Methods

Graphical method, substitution method, elimination method, cross-multiplication method and Cramer's Rule.

Homogeneous equations

a₁x + b₁y = 0
a₂x + b₂y = 0

The chapter also applies simultaneous equations to word problems.

✍️ 2. Solved Examples

Example 1 — Substitution

x + y = 7,   2x − y = 5

y = 7 − x. Therefore 2x − (7 − x) = 5 → 3x = 12 → x = 4.

Hence y = 3. Answer: (4,3)

Example 2 — Elimination

2x + 3y = 13,   4x − 3y = 5

Add: 6x = 18 → x = 3. Substitution gives y = 7/3.

Example 3 — Graphical idea

The common point of two intersecting lines is the common solution of the two equations.

⚠️ 7. Common Mistakes

Check signs carefully when transposing terms.
In elimination, multiply the complete equation when required.
Do not confuse parallel lines with coincident lines.
Always verify the final x and y in both original equations.
Define variables clearly before forming equations from a word problem.

⚡ 8. Quick Revision

Unique solution

Cramer's Rule
