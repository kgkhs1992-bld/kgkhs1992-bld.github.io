Class IX Geometry

Topic 4 — AREAS

📝 Study Material

1. Area

Area measures the region enclosed by a plane figure. The chapter develops area relationships using bases, heights, triangles and parallelograms.

2. Height of a Triangle

The height of a triangle is the perpendicular distance from a vertex to the line containing the opposite side.

Area of a triangle = ½ × base × corresponding height

3. Height of a Parallelogram

The height of a parallelogram is the perpendicular distance between its base and the opposite parallel side.

Area of a parallelogram = base × corresponding height

4. Same Base and Same Parallels

Parallelograms on the same base and between the same parallels have equal areas.

Triangles on the same base and between the same parallels also have equal areas.

5. Triangle and Parallelogram on the Same Base

When a triangle and a parallelogram stand on the same base and lie between the same parallels, the triangle has half the area of the parallelogram.

Area of triangle = ½ × area of the corresponding parallelogram

6. Equal Areas and Equal Bases

Triangles with equal areas and equal bases have equal corresponding altitudes.

7. Equal-Area Figures on the Same Base

Triangles of equal area situated on the same base and on the same side of it lie between the same parallels.

8. Quick Examples

  • Base 12 cm and height 9 cm: triangle area = ½×12×9 = 54 cm².
  • Base 15 cm and height 8 cm: parallelogram area = 15×8 = 120 cm².
  • If a triangle is on the same base and between the same parallels as a parallelogram of area 200 cm², triangle area = 100 cm².
