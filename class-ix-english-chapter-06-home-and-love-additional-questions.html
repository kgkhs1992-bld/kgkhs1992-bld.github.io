Additional Questions

One-mark practice questions.

  1. Name the poet.
    Answer: Robert William Service.
  2. Give the poet's birth year.
    Answer: 1874.
  3. Give the poet's death year.
    Answer: 1958.
  4. What are the two sweetest words in the poem?
    Answer: Home and Love.
  5. What does home represent?
    Answer: A place of belonging, care and family life.
  6. What does love represent?
    Answer: Affection and emotional connection.
  7. Complete: Home without Love is _____.
    Answer: Bitterness.
  8. Complete: Love without Home is often _____.
    Answer: Pain.
  9. What phrase means closely connected?
    Answer: Hand and glove.
  10. Who are mentioned as speaking in Heaven?
    Answer: Angels.
  11. What does gracious mean?
    Answer: Pleasant and sweet.
  12. What does tenderly mean?
    Answer: With gentle care.
  13. What does bitterness mean?
    Answer: Anger or resentment.
  14. What does seldom mean?
    Answer: Rarely.
  15. What does divine mean?
    Answer: Associated with God or the divine.
  16. What is the central idea?
    Answer: Home and Love belong together.
  17. Why are Home and Love linked?
    Answer: Because they are the poem's central ideas.
  18. Does the poet prefer one over the other?
    Answer: No; the poem treats them as complementary.
  19. What does the poem say about having both?
    Answer: Joy and gratitude.
  20. What does poverty matter in the poem's message?
    Answer: It does not make love or home unnecessary.
  21. Why does the poet repeat the key words?
    Answer: It emphasizes their importance.
  22. What feeling does the poem create?
    Answer: Warmth and affection.
  23. What is the poem's attitude to family?
    Answer: Love and family care.
  24. What is meant by 'wide and gracious range of speech'?
    Answer: It suggests language has a broad and generous expressive range.
  25. Why are the words described as sweet?
    Answer: Because they express deep tenderness.
  26. What does 'hand in glove' suggest?
    Answer: Close connection.
  27. What does the poet want readers to value?
    Answer: Home, love and family.
  28. Can love exist without a sense of home in the poem?
    Answer: The poem presents them as naturally connected.
  29. What kind of language is used?
    Answer: Simple, affectionate poetic language.
  30. What is the main moral lesson?
    Answer: Value both home and love.
