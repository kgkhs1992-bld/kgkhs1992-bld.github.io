Environment: Waiting for the Rain

Class VIII – Jasmine English | Questions & Answers

1. Who was Velu?

Velu was a hardworking farmer who had his own piece of land. He cultivated it season after season and worked throughout the year.

2. What did Velu hope for when he woke up?

Velu hoped that it would rain that day because the farmers had been waiting for rain for a long time.

3. Why was Velu worried at the beginning of the story?

Velu was worried because the rains had not come. The fields were dry, the earth had hardened and cracked, and he could not cultivate his land without rain.

4. How had Velu used his land in previous years?

Velu cultivated his land season after season. He harvested jowar in one season and dhal in the next. He had been doing this for nearly six years.

5. What happened to the fields when the rain did not come?

The fields lay untended. The earth became hard, crusted and cracked. The land became barren and forlorn, and the farmers lived on hope that the rain would come.

6. What did someone suggest the farmers should do?

Someone suggested that they should consult astrologers, who might tell them what would please the heavens and bring rain.

7. Why did Velu disagree with the suggestion to consult astrologers?

Velu said that rain had come every year without consulting astrologers. Therefore, he could not see how talking to astrologers would bring rain.

8. Why did Velu go to the weather office?

Velu wanted to find out when the sky would gather clouds and bring rain, so he went to the weather office in the city to ask for information.

9. What did the people at the weather office tell Velu?

They said that they could not really tell when the sky would gather clouds and bring rain. They themselves were at a loss because there were many favourable conditions but still no rain.

10. How did Velu feel when he returned to the village?

Velu felt tired and dejected. He was thirsty, and the dust made him cough and sneeze.

11. Whom did Velu meet under the large tree?

Velu met an old woman who was sitting under the tree to shelter from the sun.

12. Why was Velu unhappy with his situation?

Velu had worked hard and honestly, but without rain he could not till the land. If he could not till the land, no crop would grow, and he was worried about how he would feed his family.

13. What did the old woman mean when she said, “Perhaps you have worked too hard”?

She meant that Velu had worked continuously for years without giving the land a chance to rest. She wanted him to understand that the earth also needs rest.

14. Why did Velu find the old woman's statement difficult to understand?

Velu believed that a hardworking farmer should work continuously. He had not thought that the land itself might become tired and need a period of rest.

15. What did the old woman say about the earth?

She explained that the earth also works when people plough, sow and plant. The earth has worked for centuries and thousands of years, so it should be allowed to rest for some time.

16. What did the old woman mean by saying that Nature is a mother?

She meant that Nature cares for all living beings like a mother. She gives people, trees, water and soil what they need, including the rest they require.

17. What did Velu begin to understand?

Velu began to understand that the earth and Nature have their own needs and rhythm. He began to see that the land may also need rest.

18. What signs of rain did Velu notice while returning home?

He felt a cool breeze, then a tiny drop of rain on his shoulder. He saw clouds gathering in the distance, and the sky was growing dark. He expected lightning, thunder and rain.

19. How did Velu feel when he saw that the rain was coming?

Velu became happy and laughed. He ran home happily when he realised that the clouds were gathering and rain was about to come.

20. What is the central message of “Waiting for the Rain”?

The story highlights the close relationship between human beings and Nature. It teaches that the earth has its own rhythm and needs care and rest. People should respect Nature instead of expecting it to provide endlessly.

21. Give suitable meanings of the following words.

Crimson: deep red.

Cultivated: prepared and used land for growing crops.

Barren: unable to produce crops or vegetation.

Forlorn: lonely, sad or neglected.

Dejected: unhappy and disappointed.

Wrinkled: having lines or folds in the skin.

Ploughed: turned the soil with a plough for cultivation.

Centuries: periods of one hundred years.

22. What does the title “Waiting for the Rain” suggest?

The title reflects the farmers’ long wait for rain. Rain is essential for their fields, crops and livelihood, so the absence of rain creates anxiety and uncertainty.

Think and Reflect

A. Velu initially thinks mainly about his own work and crops. The old woman helps him think about the needs of the earth as well.

B. The story suggests that hardworking people should also understand the limits and needs of Nature.

C. The ending brings hope because the clouds gather and rain finally appears to be coming.

Quick Revision

Velu: hardworking farmer.

Problem: no rain and dry fields.

Weather office: could not predict when rain would come.

Old woman: teaches Velu that the earth also needs rest.

Ending: clouds gather, rain approaches, and Velu becomes happy.
