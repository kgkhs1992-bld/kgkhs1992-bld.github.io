Questions are based on the supplied Class IX English textbook story. Extracts are kept short and used as study cues.
Extract 1: “old and wrinkled”
Q1. How did the narrator first describe his grandmother?
Answer: He described her as old and wrinkled.
Extract 2: “my grandfather's portrait”
Q2. Where was the grandfather's portrait?
Answer: It hung above the mantelpiece in the drawing room.
Extract 3: “young and pretty”
Q3. What did people say about the grandmother's past?
Answer: They said she had once been young and pretty.
Extract 4: “short and fat”
Q4. How was the grandmother physically described?
Answer: She was short, fat and slightly bent.
Extract 5: “crisscross of wrinkles”
Q5. What was noticeable on her face?
Answer: Her face had many crossed wrinkles.
Extract 6: “spotless white”
Q6. What did she usually wear?
Answer: She wore spotless white clothes.
Extract 7: “telling the beads”
Q7. What did she do with her rosary?
Answer: She counted or told the beads of her rosary.
Extract 8: “winter landscape”
Q8. What was she compared with?
Answer: She was compared with a winter landscape in the mountains.
Extract 9: “good friends”
Q9. What was the narrator's relationship with his grandmother?
Answer: They were very close and good friends.
Extract 10: “wake me up”
Q10. What did the grandmother do each morning?

Extract 11: “morning prayer”
Q11. What did she recite while preparing him?
Answer: She recited her morning prayer.
Extract 12: “wooden slate”

Extract 16: “the scriptures”
Q16. What did the grandmother read at school?

Extract 17: “the city”
Q17. What changed their life together?

Extract 18: “motor bus”
Q18. How did the narrator travel to school in the city?
Answer: He travelled by motor bus.
Extract 19: “no dogs in the streets”
Q19. What was missing in the city?
