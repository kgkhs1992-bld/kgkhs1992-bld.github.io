The Lost Child

1-Mark Questions & Answers • 30 Questions
← Back to Class IX English
Q1. Who wrote 'The Lost Child'?
Answer: Mulk Raj Anand.
Q2. When does the story take place?
Answer: During the festival of spring.
Q3. Where is the little boy going?
Answer: He is going to a fair with his parents.
Q4. Why did the child lag behind?
Answer: He was attracted by things along the way.
Q5. What did the child first want from a shop?
Answer: A toy.
Q6. Why did the child not insist on the toy?
Answer: He knew his parents would refuse.
Q7. What did the mother show the child on the way?
Answer: A flowering mustard-field.
Q8. What colour did the mustard-field appear?
Answer: Pale like melting gold.
Q9. What insects attracted the child?
Answer: Dragon-flies.
Q10. What did the child try to catch?
Answer: A dragon-fly.
Q11. Where did the parents sit in the grove?
Answer: On the edge of a well.
Q12. What fell upon the child in the grove?
Answer: Young flowers.
Q13. What did the child gather?
Answer: The falling flower petals.
Q14. What birds did the child hear?
Answer: Doves.
Q15. What did the child shout on hearing the doves?
Answer: “The dove! The dove!”
Q16. What sweet did the child particularly want?
Answer: Burfi.
Q17. Name one sweetmeat mentioned in the story.
Answer: Gulab-jaman.
Q18. Who sold sweets at the fair?
Answer: A sweetmeat seller.
Q19. What flowers did the flower-seller offer?
Answer: Gulmohur flowers.
Q20. What did the child want from the flower-seller?
Answer: A garland of gulmohur.
Q21. What did the balloon seller carry?
Answer: A pole with colourful balloons.
Q22. What colours of balloons are mentioned?
Answer: Yellow, red, green and purple.
Q23. Who played the flute at the fair?
Answer: A snake-charmer.
Q24. What animal was with the snake-charmer?
Answer: A snake.
Q25. What did the child want to ride?
Answer: The roundabout.
Q26. What happened when the child asked to ride the roundabout?
Answer: His parents were no longer there.
Q27. Whom did the child call after becoming lost?
Answer: His mother and father.
Q28. How did the child feel after losing his parents?
Answer: He was frightened.
Q29. What happened to the child's turban while he ran?
Answer: It came untied.
Q30. What happened to his clothes?
Answer: They became muddy.
