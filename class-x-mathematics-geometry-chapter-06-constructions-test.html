📘 Teacher / Self-check Guide

Q1: Perpendicular bisectors → circumcentre → radius to a vertex → circle through all three vertices.

Q2: Join OP → midpoint of OP → auxiliary circle with that midpoint as centre → intersection points with given circle → join each to P.

Q3: Use compass opening equal to radius; mark six equal arcs/points; join consecutive points; verify six equal sides and 60° central angles.

Q4: Draw two perpendicular diameters through centre; join their four endpoints.

Q5: Use perpendicular tangents at suitable radius endpoints and join adjacent tangent intersections to form the circumscribed square.

Q6: Auxiliary ray with four equal intervals → join last point to B → draw parallels through the intermediate points.

Q7: Five equal intervals on auxiliary ray → use parallel-line method; numerical check: AP = 6 cm, PB = 9 cm.

Q8: Use the textbook's external-division construction and show the required ratio with auxiliary construction lines.
