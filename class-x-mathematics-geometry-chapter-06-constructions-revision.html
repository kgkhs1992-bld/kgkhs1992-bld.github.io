🎯 2. Construction Answer Formula

GIVEN → REQUIRED → CONSTRUCTION → FIGURE/LABELS → RESULT / VERIFICATION
  1. Given: ଯାହା ଦିଆଯାଇଛି ସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ ଲେଖ।
  2. Required: କ’ଣ ଅଙ୍କନ କରିବାକୁ ହେବ ଲେଖ।
  3. Construction: Compass/rulerର ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ step କ୍ରମରେ ଲେଖ।
  4. Figure: Points, arcs ଓ auxiliary lines label କର।
  5. Result: Construction condition verify କର।

⭕ 3. Circum-circle – Fast Revision

Key Point

Triangleର circumcentre = two side perpendicular bisectorsର intersection.

Radius

O circumcentre ହେଲେ OA = OB = OC.

Right Triangle

Hypotenuseର midpoint circumcentre ହୁଏ।
Perpendicular bisectors → O → OA as radius → circle through A, B, C

📐 4. Tangent – Fast Revision

Point of Contact

Tangent circleକୁ ଗୋଟିଏ pointରେ touch କରେ।

90° Rule

Point of contactରେ radius ⟂ tangent.

Two Tangents

Same external point P ଠାରୁ PA = PB.
OP² = OT² + PT²   (right triangle at T)

🔷 5. Inscribed Figures

Equilateral Triangle

Circle ଭିତରେ triangle; vertices circle ଉପରେ।

Square

Centre ଦେଇ perpendicular diameters → four endpoints join.

Regular Hexagon

Compass opening = radius; six equal arcs/points.
Regular hexagon central angle = 360° ÷ 6 = 60°
Inscribed regular hexagon: each side = circle radius

🔶 6. Circumscribed Figures

Meaning

Figure circleକୁ ବାହାରୁ ଘେରେ; sides circleକୁ touch କରେ।

Square

Appropriate radius endpointsରେ tangents ଦ୍ୱାରା square ଗଠନ।

Memory

Inscribed → vertices on circle.
Circumscribed → sides touch circle.

➗ 8. Equal Division

n equal parts → auxiliary rayରେ n equal intervals
  1. Original segment AB ଅଙ୍କନ କର।
  2. A ଠାରୁ auxiliary ray AX ନିଅ।
  3. Compassର fixed openingରେ required number of equal intervals mark କର।
  4. Last marked pointକୁ B ସହ join କର।
  5. Intermediate points ଠାରୁ required parallels ଅଙ୍କନ କର।

⚠️ 11. Top 10 Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Compass opening unnecessarily change କରିବା।
  2. Approximate perpendicular ଅଙ୍କନ କରିବା।
  3. Required construction arcs erase କରିବା।
  4. Point labels mismatch କରିବା।
  5. Inscribed/circumscribed ଉଲଟାଇବା।
  6. Internal/external division confuse କରିବା।
  7. Ratio m+n ଭୁଲ କରିବା।
  8. Parallel lines ଠିକ୍ ନ କରିବା।
  9. Final figure verify ନ କରିବା।
  10. Only final figure ଦେଇ steps ନ ଲେଖିବା।
