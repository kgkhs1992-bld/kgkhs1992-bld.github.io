English

Kamakshi Balasubramanian is an Indian author recognized for simple, insightful and thought-provoking storytelling. Her writings often revolve around nature, human psychology and rural Indian life. In “Waiting for the Rain,” the supplied material highlights the connection between a hardworking farmer and Mother Nature and the importance of respecting nature’s rhythm and wisdom.

Paragraph 12 — supplied portion

🇬🇧 English Explanation

“Perhaps she is right,” he thought, as he approached the village. It was already evening. The sun was low in the sky. Velu felt a cool breeze on his back. Then he felt a tiny drop on his shoulder. He looked up. The clouds were gathering in the distance. It was growing dark. Soon there would be lightning, thunder and sweet, fragrant rain. Velu ran home, laughing and happy.
