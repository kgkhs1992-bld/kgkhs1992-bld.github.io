Class VIII English – Topic 09

Unit 3: Mystery and Magic – Spectacular Wonders

Section A – Before You Read

1. Name any three natural phenomena mentioned in the lesson.
Answer: The lesson mentions the Northern Lights, the Giant's Causeway, the Great Blue Hole, the Great Barrier Reef, Mount Everest, the Grand Canyon and the Great Migration.
2. Why are such natural wonders called spectacular?
Answer: They are called spectacular because they are remarkable natural features or phenomena and are visually impressive.
3. Which natural wonder would you most like to see? Give one reason.
Answer: I would like to see the Northern Lights because the colourful lights in the sky are beautiful and fascinating.

Section B – Comprehension

1. What is the main idea of the lesson?
Answer: The lesson introduces several spectacular natural wonders of the world and describes their special features.
2. Write the names of any five wonders described in the lesson.
Answer: Northern Lights, Giant's Causeway, Great Blue Hole, Great Barrier Reef and Mount Everest.
3. What makes the Northern Lights spectacular?
Answer: The Northern Lights are spectacular because they produce beautiful, colourful lights in the night sky.
4. What is special about the Giant's Causeway?
Answer: The Giant's Causeway is famous for its unusual formation of many interlocking rock columns.
5. What is the Great Blue Hole?
Answer: The Great Blue Hole is a large marine sinkhole and a remarkable natural feature.
6. What is special about the Great Barrier Reef?
Answer: The Great Barrier Reef is a vast coral reef system and is known for its rich marine life.
7. Why is Mount Everest famous?
Answer: Mount Everest is famous for being the highest mountain above sea level.
8. What is the Grand Canyon?
Answer: The Grand Canyon is a huge canyon with spectacular rock formations and landscapes.
9. What is the Great Migration?
Answer: The Great Migration is a large-scale movement of animals from one area to another in search of food and water.

Section D – Grammar: Participles and Gerunds

1. Identify the -ing word as a gerund or participle.
Answers:
a) Swimming is good exercise. — Gerund
b) The glowing lights filled the sky. — Participle
c) Climbing mountains requires preparation. — Gerund
d) We saw a moving herd of animals. — Participle
2. Write one sentence using an -ing word as a gerund and one using it as a participle.
Answer:
Gerund: Reading is a useful habit.
Participle: The shining stars looked beautiful.

Section F – Short Answer Practice

1. Why should we protect natural wonders?
Answer: We should protect natural wonders because they are valuable parts of our natural world and support biodiversity and learning for future generations.
2. How can students help protect nature?
Answer: Students can avoid littering, save water, reduce waste, plant trees and encourage others to care for the environment.
3. Write a short note on “Nature is wonderful”.
Answer: Nature has many beautiful and extraordinary places and phenomena. Mountains, reefs, canyons, oceans and wildlife show the richness of our planet. We should enjoy these wonders responsibly and protect them for the future.

Section G – Think and Reflect

1. Which natural wonder impressed you most and why?
Sample Answer: The Great Barrier Reef impressed me most because of its huge coral ecosystem and rich marine life.
2. What lesson do we learn from the spectacular wonders of nature?
Answer: We learn that nature is diverse and extraordinary and that human beings have a responsibility to protect it.
