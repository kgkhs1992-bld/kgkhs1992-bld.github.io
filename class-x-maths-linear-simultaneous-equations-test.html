📝 Test Questions

Question 1

Solve: x + y = 7
x − y = 1

✅ Show Answer
x = 4, y = 3

Question 2

Solve by elimination: 3x + 2y = 16
3x − 2y = 8

✅ Show Answer
x = 4, y = 2

Question 3

Solve: 2x + 3y = 13
4x − 3y = 5

✅ Show Answer
x = 3, y = 7/3

Question 4

Determine whether the following pair has one solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions:

2x + 4y = 8
x + 2y = 5

✅ Show Answer
The pair has no solution because the two equations represent parallel lines.

Question 5

Determine the nature of the solutions:

2x + 4y = 8
x + 2y = 4

✅ Show Answer
The pair has infinitely many solutions because both equations represent the same line.

Question 6

Solve using the substitution method: x + y = 5 and x − y = 1.

✅ Show Answer
From x + y = 5, x = 5 − y.

Substituting in x − y = 1:
5 − y − y = 1
2y = 4
y = 2
Therefore, x = 3.

Answer: x = 3, y = 2

Question 7

What is the geometrical interpretation of the solution of a pair of linear equations when the two lines intersect?

✅ Show Answer
The point of intersection of the two lines gives the solution of the pair of equations.

Question 8

A number and another number have sum 15 and their difference is 3. Find the two numbers.

✅ Show Answer
Let the numbers be x and y.

x + y = 15
x − y = 3

Adding the equations:
2x = 18
x = 9
Therefore, y = 6.

The numbers are 9 and 6.
