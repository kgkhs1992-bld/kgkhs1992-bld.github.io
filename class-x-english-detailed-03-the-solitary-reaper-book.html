📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. Who is the solitary reaper?
Answer: She is a Highland girl working alone in the field.
Q2. What is the girl doing?
Answer: She is reaping and binding grain while singing.
Q3. Why does the poet ask people to listen?
Answer: Her song is beautiful and deeply moving.
Q4. Why cannot the poet understand the song?
Answer: The song is in a language he does not understand.
Q5. With what bird is the girl’s song compared?
Answer: It is compared with the nightingale and the cuckoo.
Q6. What does the nightingale comparison suggest?
Answer: It suggests that the song is soothing and beautiful.
Q7. What does the cuckoo comparison suggest?
Answer: It suggests that the song is powerful and thrilling.
Q8. What subjects does the poet imagine in her song?
Answer: He imagines sorrow, loss, old memories and battles or events of the past.
Q9. Where does the poet hear the song?
Answer: He hears it in a Highland valley while the girl works.
Q10. What remains with the poet after he leaves?
Answer: The melody remains in his heart and memory.
Q11. Who wrote The Solitary Reaper?
Answer: William Wordsworth wrote it.
Q12. What does solitary mean?
Answer: It means alone or single.
Q13. What does reaping mean?
Answer: It means cutting and gathering grain.
Q14. Why is the girl called a Highland lass?
Answer: She is a young girl living and working in the Scottish Highlands.
Q15. What is the central experience of the poem?
Answer: The poet is deeply affected by a song whose words he cannot understand.
