Book Q & A

Original KGK answers based on the Class IX textbook chapter.

1. Why is project writing included in the English book?
Answer: To meet practical communicative needs.
2. What is the first step in a project?
Answer: Select and clearly define the topic.
3. Why are objectives needed?
Answer: They explain what the project intends to find or achieve.
4. What is research?
Answer: Systematic collection of relevant information.
5. Name two possible sources of information.
Answer: Books and interviews.
6. Why should information be organized?
Answer: So that readers can understand it clearly and logically.
7. What is a conclusion?
Answer: A brief statement of the main findings or learning.
8. Why should sources be acknowledged?
Answer: To show where information came from and give proper credit.
9. What is a project title?
Answer: A clear name that identifies the project topic.
10. Why are headings useful?
Answer: They divide information into understandable sections.
11. What can tables do in a project?
Answer: Present information in a compact, organized form.
12. Why may illustrations be used?
Answer: They can make relevant information clearer.
13. What is an objective?
Answer: A specific purpose of the project.
14. What is a method section?
Answer: A description of how information was collected.
15. Why should a project avoid irrelevant information?
Answer: It keeps the work focused and useful.
16. What is an acknowledgement?
Answer: A note expressing thanks to people or sources that helped.
17. What makes a project readable?
Answer: Clear language, headings and orderly presentation.
18. What is the role of findings?
Answer: They state what the investigation or collection of information revealed.
19. What should a student do before writing?
Answer: Plan the topic and information to be collected.
20. What is the main skill developed through project work?
Answer: Practical communication, research, organization and presentation.
