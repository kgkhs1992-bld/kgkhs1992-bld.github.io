📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. Where did Lencho live?
Answer: Lencho lived in a house on the crest of a low hill.
Q2. What did Lencho hope for?
Answer: He hoped for rain for his crop.
Q3. What did he say about the raindrops?
Answer: He compared the raindrops with new coins.
Q4. How did the rain change?
Answer: The rain changed into a hailstorm.
Q5. What happened to Lencho’s corn fields?
Answer: The hailstorm destroyed the cornfield.
Q6. Who did Lencho have faith in?
Answer: He had complete faith in God.
Q7. Who did he write a letter to?
Answer: He wrote a letter to God.
Q8. Who read the letter?
Answer: The postmaster read it.
Q9. What did the postmaster do?
Answer: He collected money from the employees and his own salary and sent it to Lencho.
Q10. Was Lencho surprised to find a letter with money?
Answer: No. He was not surprised because of his strong faith in God.
Q11. What was Lencho’s reaction after getting the letter?
Answer: He counted the money, became angry about the missing amount and wrote another letter to God.
Q12. Why did Lencho keep looking at the sky?
Answer: He was waiting and hoping for rain.
Q13. Why was the field white after the storm?
Answer: It was covered with hailstones.
Q14. Why did Lencho call raindrops new coins?
Answer: He thought the rain would bring a good harvest and therefore money.
Q15. Why did Lencho prefer locusts to the storm?
Answer: He believed locusts would have left more of the crop than the destructive hailstorm.
