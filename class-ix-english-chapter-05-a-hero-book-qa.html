Book Q & A / Text Understanding

1. Who is Swami?
Answer: He is the young schoolboy at the centre of the story.
2. Why did Swami's father want him to sleep alone?
Answer: His father wanted him to overcome his fear of darkness and sleeping alone.
3. Where was Swami asked to sleep?
Answer: In his father's office room.
4. How did Swami feel when left alone?
Answer: He felt frightened and uncomfortable in the darkness.
5. What kind of dreams did he have?
Answer: He had frightening dreams and imagined a devil-like figure approaching him.
6. Why did Swami wake up?
Answer: A frightening sensation and movement in the dark disturbed his sleep.
7. What made him desperate?
Answer: He sensed someone moving near him in the darkness.
8. Who was the intruder?
Answer: A burglar.
9. Why did Swami attack the burglar?
Answer: In fear and desperation he grabbed and bit the intruder.
10. What happened when the adults arrived?
Answer: They found the injured burglar and caught him.
11. How did the school react the next day?
Answer: His classmates and teachers praised him and treated him with respect.
12. What did the Inspector suggest?
Answer: He suggested that Swami could join the police when he grew up.
13. What career did Swami actually imagine for himself?
Answer: He imagined becoming an engine driver, railway guard or bus conductor.
14. Did Swami's fear disappear completely?
Answer: No. The ending shows that he still preferred sleeping beside his grandmother.
15. What is the main idea of the story?
Answer: Courage can appear even in a frightened person when circumstances demand action.
16. What does the story say about strength and age?
Answer: It suggests that courage is not simply a matter of physical strength or age.
17. Why was Swami called a hero?
Answer: Because his unexpected action helped catch a burglar.
18. What is a 'burglar'?
Answer: A person who enters a building illegally to steal.
19. What does 'apparition' mean?
Answer: A strange figure that appears suddenly and may seem ghost-like.
20. What does 'chum' mean?
Answer: A friend.
21. What is direct speech?
Answer: The exact words of a speaker placed in quotation marks.
22. What is indirect speech?
Answer: A report of what someone said without repeating the exact original words.
23. What happens to pronouns in reported speech?
Answer: They may change according to the speaker and reporting context.
24. What happens to tense when the reporting verb is in the past?
Answer: The verb generally shifts to an appropriate past form.
25. How are yes/no questions commonly reported?
Answer: With 'if' or 'whether' followed by statement word order.
26. What did Swami's father call the boy's sleeping arrangement?
Answer: He complained about Swami returning to sleep beside his grandmother.
27. Why did Swami feel relieved at the end?
Answer: He heard that his father had given up forcing him to sleep alone.
28. What quality is celebrated by the story?
Answer: Courage.
29. Who wrote the story?
Answer: R.K. Narayan.
