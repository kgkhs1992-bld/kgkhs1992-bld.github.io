Chapter 01 – The Priceless Gift

Class IX English • Jasmine • Study Material

1. Chapter Overview

The Priceless Gift is a story about Mr. Gupta, Maggie and her mother Mrs. Bethy. It centres on family love, sympathy, hope, poverty and sacrifice.

Mr. Gupta meets Maggie in a crowded vegetarian restaurant. She is worried because her brother Franky, a soldier in India, has not written home for a long time. He later visits Maggie’s home and meets Mrs. Bethy, who believes a ring sent by Franky can reveal what a distant person is doing.

When Mrs. Bethy becomes seriously ill because of anxiety about Franky, Maggie asks Mr. Gupta to tell her mother that Franky is alive and well. He does so to comfort her. Later he learns that Franky had actually been killed in fighting on the frontier.

Before Mr. Gupta leaves for India, Maggie gives him a shilling to buy flowers for Franky’s grave. The shilling has been earned through her toil. Its value is emotional rather than monetary, making it a priceless gift.

2. Main Characters

Mr. Gupta: Narrator who becomes concerned about Maggie and her family and accepts her meaningful gift.
Maggie: A young girl who loves Franky deeply, cares for her mother and sacrifices her hard-earned shilling.
Mrs. Bethy: Maggie’s widowed mother who is anxious about her soldier son.
Franky: Maggie’s brother and Mrs. Bethy’s son, a soldier in India who is later revealed to have died in frontier fighting.

3. Important Events

  1. Mr. Gupta notices Maggie in a restaurant.
  2. He learns that she comes on Saturdays and may not earn much.
  3. He meets her again and learns about Franky.
  4. He visits Maggie’s home and meets Mrs. Bethy.
  5. Mrs. Bethy shows him Franky’s photograph and ring.
  6. Months pass without news and Mrs. Bethy becomes seriously ill.
  7. Maggie asks Mr. Gupta to reassure her mother.
  8. He tells Mrs. Bethy that Franky is alive and well, and she recovers.
  9. Mr. Gupta later learns that Franky was killed.
  10. Maggie gives him a shilling for flowers on Franky’s grave.

4. Central Idea

The story shows that a gift’s value is not measured only by money. Maggie’s shilling is priceless because it expresses love, remembrance and sacrifice.
