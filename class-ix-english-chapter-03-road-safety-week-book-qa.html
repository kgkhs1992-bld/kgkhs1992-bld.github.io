Chapter 03 – Road Safety Week

Book Q & A • Class IX English • Jasmine

Book Q & A / Text Understanding

1. What is road safety?
Answer: Road safety means protecting road users from injuries and deaths by following traffic rules and using safe practices.
2. Why is road safety important?
Answer: Because pedestrians, drivers, cyclists and passengers can all suffer serious injury or death on roads.
3. What is the main purpose of Road Safety Week?
Answer: It aims to create public awareness about traffic rules and reduce road accidents.
4. When is Road Safety Week described as being organised in the textbook?
Answer: In the month of January.
5. Who organises the national Road Safety Week programme?
Answer: The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways is named in the textbook.
6. Name two dangers the campaign warns about.
Answer: Drunken driving and high-speed driving.
7. Why should bikers wear helmets?
Answer: A helmet provides protection to the head in an accident.
8. Why should car occupants wear seat belts?
Answer: Seat belts help protect occupants during sudden braking or a crash.
9. What is distracted driving?
Answer: Driving while attention is diverted by activities such as using a phone, texting, talking to passengers or listening to loud music.
10. Why is under-age driving dangerous?
Answer: Young or unqualified drivers may lack the legal eligibility, experience and judgement required for safe driving.
11. What is one danger of drunken driving?
Answer: It can lead to rash, negligent or over-speed driving and endanger many lives.
12. What can local authorities do during the campaign?
Answer: They can check drivers and vehicles and promote compliance with safety rules.
13. How can schools participate?
Answer: They can join awareness activities, competitions, debates and other road-safety programmes.
14. Why are safety posters useful?
Answer: They communicate safety rules and warnings to road users.
15. What should drivers do near a speed-limit sign?
Answer: They should keep their speed within the indicated limit.
16. Why is phone use while driving unsafe?
Answer: It distracts the driver from the road.
17. What does a pedestrian mean?
Answer: A person travelling on foot.
18. What is a commuter?
Answer: A person who travels regularly, often between home and work.
19. What does 'paramount' mean?
Answer: More important than anything else.
20. What does 'awareness' mean?
Answer: Knowledge or understanding of something.
21. What does 'violation' mean?
Answer: Breaking a rule or law.
22. What does 'casualty' refer to in the chapter?
Answer: A person killed or injured in an accident.
23. What is a safety campaign?
Answer: A planned set of activities designed to promote safer behaviour.
24. Why are vehicle fitness certificates important?
Answer: They help ensure that vehicles used on roads are in suitable condition.
25. What should happen to people who repeatedly violate safety rules?
Answer: The chapter supports enforcement such as fines or other penalties.
26. Why are traffic signs important?
Answer: They give drivers and pedestrians information, warnings and instructions.
27. What is the final message of the chapter?
Answer: Road Safety Week reminds us to value life and protect both ourselves and others.
28. Give one rule for safe driving.
Answer: Never drink and drive.
29. Give one rule for safe riding.
Answer: Wear a helmet.
30. Give one rule for pedestrians.
Answer: Use designated crossings and remain alert.
