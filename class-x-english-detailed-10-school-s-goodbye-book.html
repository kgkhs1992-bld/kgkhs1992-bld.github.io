📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. Who is the speaker in the essay?
Answer: The speaker is addressing students who are about to leave school.
Q2. Who are the audience?
Answer: The audience is the group of school students leaving school.
Q3. What does the speaker speak about?
Answer: He speaks about education, career choice, health, character, leisure and responsible citizenship.
Q4. What wrong notion may pupils have when they say goodbye to school?
Answer: They may think that their education is finished when they leave school.
Q5. Why should education continue after leaving school?
Answer: Continued learning is necessary for success in a chosen trade or profession.
Q6. What care should be taken in choosing a profession?
Answer: A profession should be chosen carefully, with attention to future interest and suitability.
Q7. What should you do if forced to take a job you do not like?
Answer: Try to move to a suitable occupation as soon as possible.
Q8. Whom should you ask for advice about a profession?
Answer: You should seek advice from your teachers.
Q9. What prevents someone from achieving success?
Answer: Idleness, poor health, harmful habits, unsuitable choices and lack of continued learning can hinder success.
Q10. What is essential for success in life?
Answer: The essay emphasizes hard work, continued learning, good health and good character.
Q11. How can you keep in good health?
Answer: Maintain cleanliness, fresh air, regular habits and suitable recreation, and avoid harmful habits.
Q12. What should we avoid to remain healthy?
Answer: We should avoid things that weaken health, including smoking and drink.
Q13. Why is character called a priceless possession?
Answer: Because good character influences how a person lives and treats others.
Q14. How should leisure hours be used?
Answer: They should be filled with useful hobbies, good books and healthy companionship.
Q15. What makes a person a good citizen?
Answer: A sound mind and body, good character, courteous manners and loyalty to national duties and ideals.
