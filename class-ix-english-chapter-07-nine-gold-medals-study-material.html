Chapter 07 – Nine Gold Medals

KGK HIGH SCHOOL BELDANDIA

Study Material • Class IX English • Jasmine

Chapter Study Material

David Roth's poem describes a race in which nine differently abled athletes take part. One runner falls, and the other eight stop to help him. They finish together, turning the race into a powerful lesson about empathy and sportsmanship.

Important Points

Poet: David Roth.
Event: A hundred-yard race at a Special Olympics event.
Athletes: Nine runners.
Turning point: The smallest runner stumbles and falls.
Response: The other eight runners return, help him stand and walk with him.
Ending: All nine cross the finish line together and receive gold medals.
Message: Humanity, cooperation and empathy can be more important than individual victory.
