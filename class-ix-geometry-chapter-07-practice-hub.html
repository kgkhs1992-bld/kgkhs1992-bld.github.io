📝 5-Mark Questions

1. Define sin θ, cos θ and tan θ for a right-angled triangle.
Answer: sinθ=P/H, cosθ=B/H, tanθ=P/B.
2. Write the six trigonometric ratios.
Answer: sin, cos, tan, cot, sec and cosec.
3. Prove that sinθ·cosecθ=1.
Answer: Using sinθ=P/H and cosecθ=H/P, their product is 1.
4. If sinθ=3/5, find cosθ and tanθ for an acute angle.
Answer: From a 3-4-5 triangle, cosθ=4/5 and tanθ=3/4.
5. Find all six ratios when tanθ=3/4.
Answer: Taking P=3k, B=4k, H=5k: sin=3/5, cos=4/5, tan=3/4, cot=4/3, sec=5/4, cosec=5/3.
