⭐ Additional Questions & Answers

Extra practice questions prepared from the chapter content.

Q1. Why does the author connect education with success?
Answer: He says that success requires continued learning and hard work.
Q2. Why is career choice treated carefully?
Answer: A profession can shape a person’s future, so it should be selected thoughtfully.
Q3. How do hobbies contribute to life?
Answer: They can make leisure useful and provide a positive influence.
Q4. What does “make provision for hard times” mean?
Answer: It means preparing for difficulties or periods when circumstances are less favourable.
Q5. Why should young people avoid gambling?
Answer: The essay describes it as an attempt to gain something at another person’s cost.
Q6. What is meant by courteous manners?
Answer: They are polite and respectful ways of behaving toward others.
Q7. How is character connected with citizenship?
Answer: The essay connects honesty, fairness, kindness and courage with being a good citizen.
Q8. Why is the company one keeps important?
Answer: The people around us can influence our habits, attitudes and conduct.
Q9. What is the final message of the essay?
Answer: Leaving school is the beginning of wider responsibilities, not the end of education.
Q10. Why is the title suitable?
Answer: The essay is a farewell message to students leaving school and entering wider life.
