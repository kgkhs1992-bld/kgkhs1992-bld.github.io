⭐ Additional Questions & Answers

Extra practice questions prepared from the chapter content.

Q1. Why is personification important in the poem?
Answer: It turns natural objects into lively characters and creates a childlike imaginative world.
Q2. How does the poem connect flowers with children?
Answer: Both are shown as learners, playful beings and members of a larger family.
Q3. What does the holiday image suggest?
Answer: It suggests freedom, joy and movement after a period of restraint.
Q4. Why are colours important?
Answer: They make the picture of the flowers emerging after rain vivid and lively.
Q5. How does the monsoon contribute to the poem?
Answer: The rain provides the event that brings the flowers into their imagined holiday.
Q6. What does the sky represent in the poem?
Answer: It is imagined as the flowers’ wider home or world.
Q7. How does the poem appeal to children?
Answer: It transforms familiar nature into an imaginative school story.
Q8. What is the mood of the poem?
Answer: The mood is playful, imaginative and joyful.
Q9. What literary device is central to the poem?
Answer: Personification is central.
Q10. Why is the title suitable?
Answer: The poem directly imagines flowers attending a school, making “The Flower-School” an appropriate title.
