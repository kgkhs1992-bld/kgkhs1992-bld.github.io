The Beggar

Class X English • Non-Detailed Text • 50 One-Mark Questions & Answers

← Back to Class X English
1. Who wrote 'The Beggar'?
Answer: Anton Chekhov.
2. Who is the advocate in the story?
Answer: Sergei.
3. Who is the beggar?
Answer: Lushkoff.
4. What did Lushkoff claim at first?
Answer: That he was a village school teacher.
5. For how long did he claim to have had nothing to eat?
Answer: Three days.
6. How much money did Lushkoff say he lacked for lodging?
Answer: Five copecks.
7. What other job did Lushkoff later claim to have held?
Answer: He claimed to have been a student.
8. Where did Sergei remember seeing Lushkoff?
Answer: In Sadovya Street.
9. What did Sergei accuse Lushkoff of?
Answer: Lying.
10. What did Lushkoff admit about his stories?
Answer: They were lies.
11. What had Lushkoff actually done before begging?
Answer: He had sung in a Russian choir.
12. Why had Lushkoff been dismissed from the choir?
Answer: Because of drunkenness.
13. What did Sergei tell Lushkoff to do?
Answer: Work.
14. What work did Sergei offer him first?
Answer: Chopping wood.
15. Did Lushkoff refuse the offer completely?
Answer: No.
16. What was Lushkoff's condition when he began working?
Answer: He was poor and alcoholic.
17. Who helped Lushkoff change his life?
Answer: Sergei and Sergei's cook.
18. What kind of work did Lushkoff later do?
Answer: He worked honestly and became a respectable worker.
19. What happened to Lushkoff after he stopped begging?
Answer: He became a notary's assistant.
20. Where did Lushkoff meet Sergei later?
Answer: In a theatre-related setting while working.
21. What was Lushkoff's later occupation?
Answer: A notary's assistant.
22. What did Lushkoff say had changed his life?
Answer: The words and noble deeds of Sergei's cook.
23. What did the cook do for Lushkoff?
Answer: She helped him and encouraged him to change.
24. What did Sergei want Lushkoff to stop doing?
Answer: Begging and lying.
25. What drink troubled Lushkoff's life?
Answer: Vodka.
26. What did Sergei threaten when Lushkoff lied?
Answer: To send for the police.
27. What was Lushkoff ashamed to do?
Answer: Ask for help.
28. What did Lushkoff say about work?
Answer: He said he would not refuse work.
29. What was Sergei's attitude toward begging?
Answer: He urged Lushkoff to work.
30. What was Lushkoff's real problem?
Answer: Alcoholism and an unwillingness to live honestly.
31. What did Sergei offer Lushkoff instead of money?
Answer: Work.
32. What kind of man was Sergei?
Answer: An advocate who tried to reform Lushkoff.
33. What was Lushkoff's first appearance like?
Answer: Ragged and poor.
34. What color was Lushkoff's overcoat described as?
Answer: Fawn-coloured.
35. How were Lushkoff's eyes described?
Answer: Dull and drunken.
36. What did Sergei notice about Lushkoff's shoes?
Answer: They were mismatched.
37. What did Lushkoff need money for when he claimed to have a new position?
Answer: Travel to the province of Kaluga.
38. What did Sergei think of Lushkoff's lying?
Answer: He considered it dishonest.
39. What did Lushkoff say about telling the truth?
Answer: He said people would give him nothing if he told the truth.
40. What did Lushkoff eventually become?
Answer: A respectable worker.
41. What did Lushkoff thank Sergei for?
Answer: His help and kind words.
42. What did Lushkoff say about Sergei's cook?
Answer: She had helped change his heart.
43. What did Lushkoff stop doing?
Answer: Drinking.
44. What was the central change in Lushkoff's life?
Answer: He moved from begging and drinking to honest work.
45. What does the story emphasize?
Answer: The power of compassion, work and reform.
46. Who was responsible for Lushkoff's change according to Lushkoff?
Answer: Sergei's cook.
47. What did Sergei repeatedly tell Lushkoff?
Answer: To work hard and stop drinking.
48. What did Lushkoff do when he met Sergei later?
Answer: He thanked him.
49. What was Lushkoff's earlier occupation in his own confession?
Answer: A singer in a Russian choir.
50. What is the main theme of the story?
Answer: Reformation through human kindness and honest work.
