Chapter 07 – Nine Gold Medals

KGK HIGH SCHOOL BELDANDIA

Additional Questions • Class IX English • Jasmine

Additional Questions

One-mark practice questions.

  1. Name the poet.
    Answer: David Roth.
  2. How many athletes ran the race?
    Answer: Nine.
  3. What medals are mentioned in the opening stanza?
    Answer: Gold, silver and bronze.
  4. What event were they preparing for?
    Answer: A running race.
  5. Who gathered around the field?
    Answer: Spectators.
  6. Why were spectators excited?
    Answer: The final event was approaching.
  7. What were the runners poised for?
    Answer: The starting signal.
  8. What signalled the start?
    Answer: The pistol.
  9. What happened to the smallest runner?
    Answer: He stumbled and fell.
  10. Where did he fall?
    Answer: On the track/asphalt.
  11. How did he feel?
    Answer: He felt frustrated and anguished.
  12. What happened to his dreams?
    Answer: He thought his hopes were lost.
  13. How many runners returned to help him?
    Answer: Eight.
  14. Why did they return?
    Answer: To help the fallen runner.
  15. What did they do first?
    Answer: They returned to him.
  16. What did they do next?
    Answer: They helped him stand.
  17. Did they continue running at full speed?
    Answer: No.
  18. What did they do instead?
    Answer: They walked together.
  19. What did they hold?
    Answer: They joined hands.
  20. What did the banner say?
    Answer: Special Olympics.
  21. How did the race end?
    Answer: All nine crossed together.
  22. What did the spectators give them?
    Answer: A standing ovation.
  23. What is a standing ovation?
    Answer: Strong applause from standing spectators.
  24. Why did all nine receive gold medals?
    Answer: They acted with exceptional sportsmanship and mutual help.
  25. What does athlete mean?
    Answer: A participant in a sport.
  26. What does spectators mean?
    Answer: People watching an event.
  27. What does approaching mean?
    Answer: Drawing nearer.
  28. What does resolved mean?
    Answer: Determined.
  29. What does poised mean?
    Answer: Ready.
  30. What does stumble mean?
    Answer: To lose balance and fall.
  31. What does anguish mean?
    Answer: Terrible emotional pain.
  32. What does frustration mean?
    Answer: Hopelessness or distress.
  33. What does ovation mean?
    Answer: Strong public appreciation.
  34. What is the central message?
    Answer: Humanity and cooperation.
  35. What is more important than individual victory in the poem?
    Answer: Helping others.
  36. What quality is shown by the eight runners?
    Answer: Empathy and fellow feeling.
  37. Why is the title Nine Gold Medals appropriate?
    Answer: The title reflects the nine shared awards.
  38. What does the Special Olympics banner emphasize?
    Answer: It emphasizes inclusion and shared achievement.
  39. What does holding hands symbolize?
    Answer: Unity and cooperation.
  40. What does the poem teach about competition?
    Answer: Competition should include respect.
  41. What does the poem teach about cooperation?
    Answer: Helping others can be more valuable than individual victory.
  42. What does the poem teach about empathy?
    Answer: Understanding others' feelings.
  43. What kind of sportsmanship is shown?
    Answer: Supportive and humane sportsmanship.
  44. How did the fallen runner's problem change the race?
    Answer: The race became a walk for everyone.
  45. Why are the nine faces described as beaming?
    Answer: Their faces showed happiness and satisfaction.
  46. What did the athletes demonstrate beyond physical ability?
    Answer: They demonstrated compassion.
  47. What does the poem say about human dignity?
    Answer: It shows equal dignity and mutual respect.
  48. What value should students learn from the athletes?
    Answer: Students should help others.
  49. What is the emotional effect of the ending?
    Answer: The ending is inspiring and emotional.
  50. Give the poem's message in one sentence.
    Answer: Humanity can turn competition into cooperation.
