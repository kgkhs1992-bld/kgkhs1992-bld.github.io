Class IX Geometry

Topic 3 — QUADRILATERAL

📝 Study Material

1. Quadrilateral

A quadrilateral is a polygon with four sides. Its four sides, four angles and four vertices are named in order.

2. Convex Quadrilateral

A convex quadrilateral has all its interior angles less than 180°. Its diagonals lie inside the quadrilateral.

3. Parts of a Quadrilateral

4. Polygon

A polygon is a closed plane figure formed by line segments. A polygon has at least 3 sides.

5. Names by Number of Sides

  • 3 — Triangle
  • 4 — Quadrilateral
  • 5 — Pentagon
  • 6 — Hexagon
  • 7 — Heptagon
  • 8 — Octagon
  • 9 — Nonagon
  • 10 — Decagon

6. Convex Polygon

A polygon is convex when its interior angles are less than 180° and its diagonals lie inside the polygon.

7. Regular Polygon

A regular polygon has equal sides and equal angles.

Sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon = (n − 2) × 180°
Sum of exterior angles of any polygon = 360°
Each exterior angle of a regular n-sided polygon = 360° / n
Each interior angle of a regular n-sided polygon = (n − 2) × 180° / n

8. Quick Examples

  • Quadrilateral: (4−2)×180° = 360°.
  • Pentagon: (5−2)×180° = 540°.
  • Hexagon: (6−2)×180° = 720°.
  • For a regular polygon with exterior angle 40°, n = 360°/40° = 9.
