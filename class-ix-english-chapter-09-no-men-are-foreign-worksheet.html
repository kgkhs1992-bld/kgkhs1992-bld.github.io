Chapter 09 – No Men Are Foreign

Worksheet – 50 × 1 Mark

Chapter-based one-mark practice with answers.

1. Who wrote No Men Are Foreign?
Answer: James Kirkup.
2. What is the main theme?
Answer: The essential unity and equality of human beings.
3. What does the poet say about countries?
Answer: No country should be treated as inherently foreign or strange.
4. What is beneath all uniforms?
Answer: A single human body breathes.
5. What do all people share?
Answer: Sun, air, water, work, feelings and the earth.
6. What does the word Remember emphasize?
Answer: The need to keep human unity in mind.
7. How are people's hands described?
Answer: They are like ours and show similar labour.
8. How can strength be won?
Answer: By love.
9. What happens when people hate their brothers?
Answer: They ultimately harm themselves and humanity.
10. What do wars defile?
Answer: The human earth and the shared environment.
11. Who is the poet?
Answer: James Kirkup.
12. When was Kirkup born?
Answer: 23 April 1918.
13. When did he die?
Answer: 10 May 2009.
14. What award did he win?
Answer: The Atlantic Award for Literature in 1950.
15. What does the poem say about human labour?
Answer: People everywhere work for life in similar ways.
16. Why is the title appropriate?
Answer: It expresses the poem's message that no people or countries should be regarded as foreign or strange.
17. What does earth symbolize?
Answer: The common home of humanity.
18. What does the poem oppose?
Answer: Hatred, war and divisions that deny common humanity.
19. What is the role of repetition?
Answer: It reinforces the central message and makes it memorable.
20. What is the final message?
Answer: Human beings share one humanity and should not hate one another.
21. What is a key word used repeatedly in this chapter?
Answer: The answer depends on the chapter; students should identify it from the text.
22. What is the chapter mainly designed to develop?
Answer: Reading, understanding and communicative English skills.
23. What should a student do when a question asks for evidence?
Answer: Use relevant details from the text.
24. Why are glossary words useful?
Answer: They help understand difficult vocabulary.
25. What is comprehension?
Answer: Understanding and responding to what is read.
26. What is the purpose of a short answer?
Answer: To give the required information clearly and directly.
27. What is the benefit of revising a chapter?
Answer: It improves understanding and recall.
28. What should a good answer contain?
Answer: Relevant information expressed clearly.
29. Why should students read questions carefully?
Answer: To identify exactly what information is required.
30. What is a main idea?
Answer: The central point of a text.
31. What is a supporting detail?
Answer: A fact or example that explains a main idea.
32. What is a theme?
Answer: A central idea or message developed by a literary work.
33. What is a glossary?
Answer: A list explaining difficult words and terms.
34. What is a poem?
Answer: A literary composition arranged in lines and often using rhythm and imagery.
35. What is prose?
Answer: Ordinary written language arranged in sentences and paragraphs.
36. What is a source?
Answer: A place from which information is obtained.
37. Why should students avoid copying without understanding?
Answer: Understanding is necessary for meaningful learning.
38. What is a summary?
Answer: A brief statement of the main points.
39. What is inference?
Answer: A conclusion reached from evidence in the text.
40. What is revision?
Answer: Reviewing learned material to strengthen understanding and memory.
41. How can a student improve vocabulary?
Answer: By reading, learning meanings and using new words in context.
42. Why is clear handwriting important?
Answer: It makes written answers easier to read.
43. What is the safest way to answer a one-mark question?
Answer: Give the precise fact or phrase required.
44. What should be checked before submitting a worksheet?
Answer: Spelling, completeness and correctness.
45. Why are headings useful in notes?
Answer: They organize information.
46. What is a learning outcome?
Answer: What a student is expected to know or be able to do.
47. What is a factual question?
Answer: A question answered directly from information in the text.
48. What is a value-based question?
Answer: A question asking the learner to reflect on an idea or value.
49. What is a text-based answer?
Answer: An answer supported by details from the prescribed text.
50. What is the benefit of making chapter notes?
Answer: They make revision quicker and clearer.
