Book Q & A

Original KGK answers based on the Class IX textbook chapter.

1. Who wrote No Men Are Foreign?
Answer: James Kirkup.
2. What is the main theme?
Answer: The essential unity and equality of human beings.
3. What does the poet say about countries?
Answer: No country should be treated as inherently foreign or strange.
4. What is beneath all uniforms?
Answer: A single human body breathes.
5. What do all people share?
Answer: Sun, air, water, work, feelings and the earth.
6. What does the word Remember emphasize?
Answer: The need to keep human unity in mind.
7. How are people's hands described?
Answer: They are like ours and show similar labour.
8. How can strength be won?
Answer: By love.
9. What happens when people hate their brothers?
Answer: They ultimately harm themselves and humanity.
10. What do wars defile?
Answer: The human earth and the shared environment.
11. Who is the poet?
Answer: James Kirkup.
12. When was Kirkup born?
Answer: 23 April 1918.
13. When did he die?
Answer: 10 May 2009.
14. What award did he win?
Answer: The Atlantic Award for Literature in 1950.
15. What does the poem say about human labour?
Answer: People everywhere work for life in similar ways.
16. Why is the title appropriate?
Answer: It expresses the poem's message that no people or countries should be regarded as foreign or strange.
17. What does earth symbolize?
Answer: The common home of humanity.
18. What does the poem oppose?
Answer: Hatred, war and divisions that deny common humanity.
19. What is the role of repetition?
Answer: It reinforces the central message and makes it memorable.
20. What is the final message?
Answer: Human beings share one humanity and should not hate one another.
