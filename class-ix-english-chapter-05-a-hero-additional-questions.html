Additional Questions

One-mark practice questions.

  1. Name the main character.
    Answer: Swami.
  2. Who is Swami's father?
    Answer: Swami's father.
  3. Where did Swami usually sleep?
    Answer: Beside his grandmother.
  4. Why did father change the sleeping arrangement?
    Answer: To make Swami overcome fear.
  5. What frightened Swami?
    Answer: Darkness and being alone.
  6. Where was the office room?
    Answer: His father's office room.
  7. What did Swami imagine in the dark?
    Answer: A frightening devil-like figure.
  8. What sound or movement disturbed him?
    Answer: A movement or presence in the dark.
  9. Who entered the office?
    Answer: A burglar.
  10. Who was the intruder?
    Answer: A burglar.
  11. What did Swami do to the intruder?
    Answer: He attacked and bit him.
  12. Who came carrying a light?
    Answer: Father, cook and a servant.
  13. What happened to the burglar?
    Answer: The burglar was caught.
  14. How did Swami's classmates react?
    Answer: They respected him.
  15. Who praised Swami at school?
    Answer: His teacher and headmaster praised him.
  16. What did the Inspector suggest?
    Answer: Join the police when he grew up.
  17. What job did Swami really prefer?
    Answer: Engine driver, railway guard or bus conductor.
  18. Did Swami enjoy sleeping alone?
    Answer: No.
  19. What does 'fixedly' mean?
    Answer: Continuously without looking away.
  20. What does 'sneer' mean?
    Answer: To show disrespect through expression.
  21. What does 'mumble' mean?
    Answer: To speak unclearly in a low voice.
  22. What does 'gazing' mean?
    Answer: Looking steadily.
  23. What does 'gloomily' mean?
    Answer: Depressingly.
  24. What does 'apparition' mean?
    Answer: A strange ghost-like figure.
  25. What does 'monster' mean?
    Answer: An abnormal or frightening person.
  26. What does 'chum' mean?
    Answer: A friend.
  27. What does 'armour' mean?
    Answer: Protective metal clothing.
  28. What does 'crouched' mean?
    Answer: To crouch with limbs drawn close.
  29. What does 'encased' mean?
    Answer: Completely covered.
  30. What does 'thud' mean?
    Answer: A heavy dull sound.
  31. What does 'shreds' mean?
    Answer: Very small pieces.
  32. What is a noun?
    Answer: A word naming a person, place, thing or idea.
  33. What is a verb?
    Answer: A word expressing an action or state.
  34. Can one English word function as noun and verb?
    Answer: Yes.
  35. What is direct speech?
    Answer: Repeating the speaker's exact words.
  36. What is indirect speech?
    Answer: Reporting the meaning without exact words.
  37. What punctuation marks direct speech?
    Answer: Quotation marks.
  38. What word can introduce a reported yes/no question?
    Answer: If or whether.
  39. Why can pronouns change in reported speech?
    Answer: They must agree with the new speaker/subject.
  40. What happens to tense with a past reporting verb?
    Answer: It generally shifts to an appropriate past form.
  41. What is courage?
    Answer: The ability to act despite fear.
  42. Does courage always mean absence of fear?
    Answer: No.
  43. Why is Swami's action surprising?
    Answer: He was known as timid but acted under pressure.
  44. What did his father want him to learn?
    Answer: The lesson about courage.
  45. What did the incident change?
    Answer: Fear can coexist with brave action.
  46. Why did Swami feel relieved?
    Answer: Age does not by itself determine courage.
  47. What does the story teach about fear?
    Answer: The incident changed how others viewed him.
  48. What does the story teach about age?
    Answer: He was allowed to return to his grandmother's sleeping place.
  49. What is the central message of A Hero?
    Answer: Courage can appear unexpectedly.
