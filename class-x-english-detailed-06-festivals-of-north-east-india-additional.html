⭐ Additional Questions & Answers

Extra practice questions prepared from the chapter content.

Q1. How do agricultural cycles influence festivals?
Answer: Many festivals mark sowing, growing or harvesting periods.
Q2. Why are traditional musical instruments important?
Answer: They accompany songs and dances and help preserve cultural practices.
Q3. How can festivals strengthen community life?
Answer: They bring people together in shared celebrations and traditions.
Q4. What does cultural diversity mean in this context?
Answer: It means different communities have their own languages, customs, festivals, music and practices.
Q5. Why should traditional festivals be preserved?
Answer: Preserving them helps maintain cultural heritage for future generations.
Q6. How can students learn from regional festivals?
Answer: They can learn to appreciate different traditions and understand cultural diversity.
Q7. What is common among many festivals described?
Answer: Many involve community participation, traditional food, music, dance or agriculture.
Q8. How are festivals connected with nature?
Answer: Several are timed according to seasons, farming activities and harvests.
Q9. Why is the North-East described through festivals?
Answer: Festivals provide a visible way to understand the region’s diverse cultural life.
Q10. What is one major educational value of the lesson?
Answer: It encourages knowledge and appreciation of India’s regional cultural diversity.
