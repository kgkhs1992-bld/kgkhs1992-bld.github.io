Chapter 01 – Linear Simultaneous Equations

Class X Mathematics

📚 Chapter at a Glance

Revise the important concepts, methods, formulas and steps before solving problems.

1️⃣ What is a Linear Simultaneous Equation?

Two linear equations containing the same two variables are called a pair of linear simultaneous equations.

ax + by = c

dx + ey = f

Here x and y are the variables.

2️⃣ Main Methods of Solving

➊ Substitution Method

Find one variable in terms of the other and substitute it into the second equation.

➋ Elimination Method

Make the coefficients of one variable equal or opposite and eliminate that variable.

➌ Graphical Method

Represent both equations as straight lines. Their point of intersection gives the solution.

3️⃣ Substitution Method – Steps

  1. Take either equation.
  2. Express one variable in terms of the other.
  3. Substitute this value into the second equation.
  4. Solve for the remaining variable.
  5. Substitute the value back to find the other variable.
  6. Check the answer in both original equations.
Example:
x + y = 9
x − y = 1

Adding the equations:
2x = 10
Therefore, x = 5
and y = 4.

4️⃣ Elimination Method – Steps

  1. Arrange both equations properly.
  2. Choose the variable to eliminate.
  3. Multiply one or both equations if necessary.
  4. Add or subtract the equations.
  5. Find one variable.
  6. Substitute it to find the other variable.
  7. Verify the solution.
2x + y = 7
3x − y = 8

Add → 5x = 15
x = 3

5️⃣ Graphical Method

Each linear equation represents a straight line. The solution depends on how the two lines meet.

Intersecting lines → One solution

Parallel lines → No solution

Same line → Infinitely many solutions

6️⃣ Important Sign Rules

+ × + = +
+ × − = −
− × + = −
− × − = +
💡 When a term is moved from one side of an equation to the other, its sign changes.

7️⃣ Checking the Solution

Suppose the solution obtained is:

x = 5,    y = 4

Put these values into both original equations. Both equations must be satisfied.

✅ Always check your final answer.

8️⃣ Word Problems – Quick Steps

  1. Read the problem carefully.
  2. Let the unknown quantities be x and y.
  3. Form the first equation.
  4. Form the second equation.
  5. Solve the simultaneous equations.
  6. Interpret the answer according to the question.
  7. Check whether the answer is reasonable.

9️⃣ Most Important Exam Tips

  • ✔️ Write equations clearly.
  • ✔️ Keep like terms together.
  • ✔️ Be careful with negative signs.
  • ✔️ Show all important steps.
  • ✔️ Use brackets when substituting negative values.
  • ✔️ Check the final values in both equations.
  • ✔️ Write the final answer clearly.

🎯 30-Second Final Revision

  • 📌 Two equations → two variables.
  • 📌 Main methods → Substitution, Elimination, Graphical.
  • 📌 Elimination → remove one variable.
  • 📌 Substitution → replace one variable.
  • 📌 Graphical → intersection gives the solution.
  • 📌 Check the answer in both original equations.
