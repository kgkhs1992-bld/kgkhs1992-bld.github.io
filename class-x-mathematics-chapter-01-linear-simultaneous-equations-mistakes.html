⚠️ Common Mistakes

Chapter 01 – Linear Simultaneous Equations

Class X Mathematics

🚫 Avoid These Common Errors

Careful working, correct signs and proper checking can help you avoid most mistakes while solving linear simultaneous equations.

1️⃣ Changing Signs Incorrectly

When a term is moved from one side of an equation to the other side, its sign changes.

❌ Wrong:

x + 5 = 12
x = 12 + 5
x = 17
✅ Correct:

x + 5 = 12
x = 12 − 5
x = 7
💡 Always check the sign when transposing a term.

2️⃣ Forgetting to Multiply the Complete Equation

In the elimination method, if an equation is multiplied by a number, every term must be multiplied.

2x + y = 5

× 3

6x + 3y = 15
❌ Do not multiply only one term.
✅ Multiply every term on both sides.

3️⃣ Making a Sign Error During Elimination

Negative signs are especially important when adding or subtracting two equations.

2x + y = 7
3x − y = 8

Add:
5x = 15
x = 3
💡 Write the equations one below the other before adding or subtracting them.

4️⃣ Substituting Without Brackets

When substituting a negative expression, brackets should be used carefully.

y = 5 − x

2x − y = 7

2x − (5 − x) = 7
❌ 2x − 5 − x = 7
without properly considering the bracket.
✅ 2x − (5 − x) = 7
= 2x − 5 + x

5️⃣ Not Checking the Final Answer

After finding the values of x and y, students sometimes stop without verification.

✅ Put the values of x and y back into both original equations.
Example:

If x = 5 and y = 4, substitute these values into both original equations.

6️⃣ Confusing No Solution and Infinite Solutions

The graphical interpretation is important.

Intersecting lines
→ One solution

Parallel distinct lines
→ No solution

Coincident lines
→ Infinitely many solutions

7️⃣ Writing the Equations Incorrectly in Word Problems

In application problems, first define the variables clearly.

✅ Let the first number be x and the second number be y.

Then translate the information in the question into two mathematical equations.

💡 Do not start calculating before forming the correct equations.

8️⃣ Confusing Variables and Constants

In an equation such as:

3x + 2y = 12

x and y are variables, while 3, 2 and 12 are constants or coefficients.

9️⃣ Using the Wrong Method Carelessly

Different pairs of equations may be easier to solve using different methods.

  • Use substitution when one variable is easily isolated.
  • Use elimination when coefficients can be cancelled easily.
  • Use graphical method when the question asks for a graph.
  • Use Cramer's Rule when determinants are appropriate.
💡 Choose the method carefully, but always show the necessary steps.

🔟 Not Writing the Final Answer Clearly

After solving, write the values of both variables clearly.

✅ Therefore,

x = 5, y = 4

For a word problem, also state what the values represent.

🎯 Final Mistake-Checking Checklist

  • ✔️ Did I copy both equations correctly?
  • ✔️ Did I handle positive and negative signs correctly?
  • ✔️ Did I multiply every term when required?
  • ✔️ Did I use brackets during substitution?
  • ✔️ Did I perform addition/subtraction correctly?
  • ✔️ Did I find both x and y?
  • ✔️ Did I check the answer in both original equations?
  • ✔️ Did I write the final answer clearly?
