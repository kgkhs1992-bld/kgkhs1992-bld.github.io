04 September 2026

🌱 The Beginning

The journey of building the KGKHS Beldandia digital presence began. The aim was to create a useful, informative and living website for the school.

Building the Foundation

💻 Pages, Design & Structure

Home, About, Academics, Staff, Gallery, Notices and other sections were developed, tested and refined.

Learning Resources

📚 More Content → More Learning

Learning materials, textbook references, study resources, questions, answers and practice sections were gradually added for students.

Student Results

📝 Results & Assessments

Student assessment data, Excel uploads, result processing and PDF result generation became part of the school's digital system.

Admin & School Files

☁️ A Living School Website

The Admin Login and Supabase-powered file system made it possible to publish school files such as notices, PDFs, images, videos and audio from the administration side.

The Sleepless Nights

🌙 When Night Became Morning

Some of the hardest problems were solved after hours of repeated testing. There were attempts that continued until nearly 5:00 AM, while the rest of the world was asleep.

30 September 2026

🏆 A Major Milestone

The successful Admin → Supabase → Website file-upload test became another memorable milestone in the journey.
