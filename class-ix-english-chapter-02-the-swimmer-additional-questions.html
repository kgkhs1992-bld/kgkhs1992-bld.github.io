Chapter 02 – The Swimmer Who Does Not Need Her Legs!

KGK HIGH SCHOOL BELDANDIA

50 One-Mark Additional Questions • Class IX English • Jasmine

50 One-Mark Additional Questions

Each question carries 1 mark.

  1. In which city did Janaki begin her hard training?
    Answer: Bangalore.
  2. In which year was Janaki struck by polio?
    Answer: 1987.
  3. How long did Janaki train before she could spend ten hours in the pool?
    Answer: Almost four years.
  4. Who gave Janaki a certificate for ten hours of continuous swimming?
    Answer: Mr. Gopal.
  5. How long did Janaki train at Malpe?
    Answer: Three weeks.
  6. What sea did Janaki train in at Malpe?
    Answer: The Arabian Sea.
  7. What minimum distance did she swim each day at Malpe?
    Answer: 20 kilometres.
  8. Where did Janaki send her certificates?
    Answer: To the Channel Swimming Association in England.
  9. When did Janaki and her parents leave for England?
    Answer: In the middle of 1992.
  10. Where did Janaki begin practising in the English Channel?
    Answer: At Dover.
  11. How long did she train under Stella Streeter?
    Answer: About three weeks.
  12. How many swimmers formed the relay team?
    Answer: Six.
  13. How long did each relay swimmer have to swim at minimum?
    Answer: Two hours.
  14. What was the relay team's name?
    Answer: Janaki's Maritime Express.
  15. When was the unique Channel attempt made?
    Answer: 28 July 1992.
  16. How long did Janaki swim in the relay?
    Answer: About two hours.
  17. How long did the team take to cross the Channel?
    Answer: 14 hours and 45 minutes.
  18. How wide was the Channel crossed by the relay team according to the lesson?
    Answer: 36 km.
  19. What was Janaki's father's name?
    Answer: Mr. Nagappa.
  20. What was Janaki's mother's name?
    Answer: Mrs. Indiramma.
  21. What did the Channel water do to Janaki's body?
    Answer: Its coldness required extra energy and the waves affected her leg movement.
  22. What happened when salty water entered Janaki's mouth?
    Answer: She felt sick.
  23. What sea creatures stuck to Janaki's body?
    Answer: Seaweeds and jellyfish.
  24. What was Janaki's occupation?
    Answer: She was a bank officer in Bangalore.
  25. Where did Janaki want to train for the 1996 Paraplegic Olympics?
    Answer: The lesson says she wanted to start training for the event in Atlanta.
  26. What does “solo” mean?
    Answer: Swimming alone.
  27. What does “at a stretch” mean?
    Answer: Without stopping.
  28. What does “morsel” mean?
    Answer: A small piece of food.
  29. What does “obstacle” mean?
    Answer: A hindrance or barrier.
  30. What does “self-reliance” mean?
    Answer: The ability to do things and make decisions by oneself.
  31. What does “NIS” stand for?
    Answer: National Institute of Sports.
  32. What does “waters” mean in the glossary?
    Answer: Parts of a sea or river.
  33. What does “agonizing” mean?
    Answer: Causing mental anguish or worry.
  34. What is an English Channel?
    Answer: The narrow sea passage separating England and France.
  35. What disease affected Janaki's legs?
    Answer: Polio.
  36. What kind of swimming is marathon swimming?
    Answer: Swimming in the high seas for long hours at a stretch.
  37. Who trained Janaki at Malpe?
    Answer: Dinesh Suvarna.
  38. Who was the famous Channel coach?
    Answer: Stella Streeter.
  39. Why was a solo attempt not arranged?
    Answer: There was no special boat to accompany Janaki.
  40. What was the main purpose of the special boat mentioned?
    Answer: It could lift a swimmer out of the water if necessary.
  41. How many hours of continuous water time was required?
    Answer: At least ten hours.
  42. What did the government and private agencies help Janaki with?
    Answer: They contributed money for her attempt.
  43. What did Janaki's teammates do when they saw her ability?
    Answer: They were amazed and honoured her by naming the relay team after her.
  44. What did Janaki say was important besides hard work and courage?
    Answer: Self-reliance.
  45. What did Janaki say about the word “impossible”?
    Answer: She believed it is applied to something that has not been tried.
  46. What does “could” generally express in the lesson?
    Answer: General ability.
  47. What does “was able to” express in the lesson?
    Answer: Ability together with successful performance in a particular situation.
  48. Give one negative prefix from the lesson.
    Answer: For example, un-, im-, in-, dis-, mis-, de- or il-.
  49. Name one phrase taught in the chapter.
    Answer: For example, “at a stretch.”
  50. What kind of letter is suggested at the end of the chapter?
    Answer: A letter asking for information about a swimming course.
