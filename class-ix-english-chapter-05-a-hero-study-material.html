Chapter 05 – A Hero

KGK HIGH SCHOOL BELDANDIA

Study Material • Class IX English • Jasmine

Chapter Study Material

R.K. Narayan's story follows Swami, a schoolboy who fears sleeping alone but unexpectedly confronts a burglar at night. The incident changes how others see him and raises questions about courage and fear.

Important Points

Main character: Swami, a schoolboy.
Conflict: His father insists that he sleep alone in the office room to overcome his fear.
Night incident: Swami wakes in darkness and struggles with fear before encountering a burglar.
Action: He bites the burglar, who is caught when the adults arrive.
Aftermath: Swami is praised as a brave boy, even though he still fears sleeping alone.
Language focus: Noun/verb uses and direct/indirect speech are practised.

Key Learning Areas

This school edition keeps the chapter's main reading, comprehension, vocabulary, language and value-education areas, but presents them in original wording for KGK HIGH SCHOOL BELDANDIA.
