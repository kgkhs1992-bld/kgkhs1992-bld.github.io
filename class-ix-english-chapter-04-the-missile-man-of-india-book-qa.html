Book Q & A / Text Understanding

1. Who was Dr A.P.J. Abdul Kalam?
Answer: He was a distinguished Indian scientist and later the 11th President of India.
2. Why was he called the Missile Man of India?
Answer: Because of his major involvement in India's missile-development work.
3. Where and when was he born?
Answer: At Rameswaram in Tamil Nadu on 15 October 1931.
4. What did his father do?
Answer: He owned a boat/ferry and served as the Imam of a local mosque.
5. How did Kalam help his family as a boy?
Answer: He sold newspapers to supplement the family income.
6. Who was his science teacher?
Answer: Sivasubramania Iyer.
7. What quality of the teacher influenced Kalam?
Answer: The teacher was loving and encouraged him to develop his abilities.
8. Where did Kalam receive secondary education?
Answer: At Schwartz Higher Secondary School.
9. Where did he graduate in physics?
Answer: At Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.
10. What was Kalam's dream?
Answer: He wanted to become a pilot.
11. How did his sister help with his engineering education?
Answer: She mortgaged her gold ornaments to help pay his admission fee.
12. Where did he complete aerospace engineering?
Answer: At the Madras Institute of Technology.
13. When did he complete aerospace engineering?
Answer: In 1960.
14. Where did he begin his career as a trainee?
Answer: At Hindustan Aeronautics Limited in Bangalore.
15. Which organisation did he join as a scientist?
Answer: DRDO.
16. When did he join ISRO?
Answer: In 1969.
17. What was the purpose of SLV-III?
Answer: To develop technology for launching satellites.
18. When was the first Rohini satellite launched successfully?
Answer: In 1980.
19. What programme did Kalam lead at DRDO?
Answer: The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme.
20. Name the four missiles mentioned in the chapter.
Answer: Prithvi, Trishul, Akash and Nag.
21. What did Kalam supervise at Pokhran?
Answer: The Pokhran-II nuclear tests.
22. What awards are mentioned?
Answer: Padma Bhushan, Padma Vibhushan and Bharat Ratna.
23. When did Kalam become President of India?
Answer: In 2002.
24. Why is he called the People's President?
Answer: The chapter says he was widely referred to by that title because of his popularity with people.
25. Where did Kalam die?
Answer: Shillong.
26. When did he die?
Answer: 27 July 2015.
27. What qualities does the chapter highlight?
Answer: Hard work, learning, determination, humility and service.
28. What is the main educational message?
Answer: Difficult circumstances need not prevent a person from pursuing learning and meaningful goals.
29. What does DRDO stand for?
Answer: Defence Research and Development Organisation.
30. What does ISRO stand for?
Answer: Indian Space Research Organisation.
