The Portrait of a Lady

1-Mark Questions & Answers • 30 Questions
← Back to Class IX English
Q1. Who wrote 'The Portrait of a Lady'?
Answer: Khushwant Singh.
Q2. Who is the central figure of the story?
Answer: The grandmother.
Q3. How did the grandmother look?
Answer: She was old and wrinkled.
Q4. How long had the narrator known his grandmother?
Answer: For twenty years.
Q5. Who was in the portrait above the mantelpiece?
Answer: The narrator's grandfather.
Q6. What did the grandfather wear in the portrait?
Answer: A big turban and loose-fitting clothes.
Q7. What covered the grandfather's chest?
Answer: A long white beard.
Q8. Where did the grandmother live with the narrator initially?
Answer: In the village.
Q9. What did the grandmother do in the village?
Answer: She looked after the narrator and accompanied him to school.
Q10. Where was the village school?
Answer: Attached to the temple.
Q11. Who went with the narrator to school?
Answer: His grandmother.
Q12. What did the grandmother carry to school?
Answer: The narrator's school things.
Q13. What did the grandmother do while the narrator studied?
Answer: She read religious books and prayed.
Q14. What happened when the narrator moved to the city?
Answer: His grandmother's daily routine changed.
Q15. Why could the grandmother not accompany him to the city school?
Answer: The city school was different and the narrator travelled to it by bus.
Q16. What did the grandmother dislike about the city school?
Answer: It taught subjects unrelated to her religious interests.
Q17. What subject particularly displeased her?
Answer: Music.
Q18. What did the grandmother feed in the city?
Answer: Sparrows.
Q19. Where did the sparrows gather?
Answer: Around the grandmother.
Q20. What did the grandmother do with the sparrows?
Answer: She fed them grains.
Q21. What happened when the narrator went to university?
Answer: He was given a separate room.
Q22. How did the grandmother spend much of her time then?
Answer: She sat at her spinning wheel and prayed.
Q23. What did the grandmother do on the narrator's departure abroad?
Answer: She went to the railway station to see him off.
Q24. What was unusual about the grandmother at the station?
Answer: She remained composed and silent.
Q25. What did the grandmother celebrate when the narrator returned?
Answer: She celebrated his return by singing and beating a drum.
Q26. What happened to the grandmother after her celebration?
Answer: She became ill.
Q27. What did the grandmother do before her death?
Answer: She prayed.
Q28. What happened to the sparrows after the grandmother's death?
Answer: They sat silently around her body and did not eat the offered bread.
Q29. What did the sparrows do when the grandmother's body was taken away?
Answer: They flew away quietly.
Q30. What is the main relationship shown in the story?
Answer: The deep bond between the narrator and his grandmother.
