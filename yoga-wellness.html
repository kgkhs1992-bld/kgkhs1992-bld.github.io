Yoga for a Healthy Body and Peaceful Mind

Yoga is a practice that combines body movements, breathing, concentration and relaxation.

ଯୋଗ ହେଉଛି ଶରୀରର ଚଳନ, ଶ୍ୱାସ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ, ଏକାଗ୍ରତା ଓ ବିଶ୍ରାମର ଏକ ସୁନ୍ଦର ଅଭ୍ୟାସ।

Regular yoga practice may help improve flexibility, balance, concentration and relaxation.

ନିୟମିତ ଯୋଗ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ ଶରୀରର ନମନୀୟତା, ସନ୍ତୁଳନ, ଏକାଗ୍ରତା ଓ ମନର ଶାନ୍ତି ବଢ଼ାଇବାରେ ସାହାଯ୍ୟ କରିପାରେ।

🌬️

1. Deep Breathing

ଗଭୀର ଶ୍ୱାସ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ

  • Sit comfortably.
  • Keep your back straight.
  • Breathe in slowly through the nose.
  • Breathe out gently.
  • Repeat without force.

ସୁବିଧାରେ ବସି ନାକ ଦ୍ୱାରା ଧୀରେ ଶ୍ୱାସ ନିଅନ୍ତୁ ଓ ଧୀରେ ଶ୍ୱାସ ଛାଡ଼ନ୍ତୁ।

🌴

2. Tadasana

ତାଡ଼ାସନ

  • Stand straight with feet together.
  • Raise both arms slowly.
  • Stretch the body upward.
  • Breathe normally.
  • Return slowly to the starting position.

ସିଧା ଛିଡ଼ା ହୋଇ ଦୁଇ ହାତ ଉପରକୁ ଉଠାଇ ଶରୀରକୁ ଉପର ଦିଗକୁ ଟାଣନ୍ତୁ।

🌳

3. Vrikshasana

ବୃକ୍ଷାସନ

  • Stand straight.
  • Balance on one leg.
  • Place the other foot carefully.
  • Join the palms near the chest.
  • Change sides slowly.

ଗୋଟିଏ ଗୋଡ଼ରେ ସନ୍ତୁଳନ ରଖି ଅନ୍ୟ ଗୋଡ଼କୁ ସାବଧାନରେ ରଖନ୍ତୁ। ଦୁଇ ପାର୍ଶ୍ୱରେ ଅଭ୍ୟାସ କରନ୍ତୁ।

🧘

4. Padmasana

ପଦ୍ମାସନ

  • Sit on the floor comfortably.
  • Keep the back straight.
  • Place the legs comfortably.
  • Rest the hands on the knees.
  • Focus on calm breathing.

ଭୂମିରେ ସୁବିଧାରେ ବସି ପିଠି ସିଧା ରଖନ୍ତୁ। ହାତକୁ ଆଣ୍ଠୁ ଉପରେ ରଖି ଶ୍ୱାସ ଉପରେ ଧ୍ୟାନ ଦିଅନ୍ତୁ।

🪷

5. Vajrasana

ବଜ୍ରାସନ

  • Kneel gently on a clean mat.
  • Sit comfortably on the heels.
  • Keep the back straight.
  • Place hands on the thighs.
  • Breathe calmly.

ସଫା ମାଟ୍‌ ଉପରେ ଆଣ୍ଠୁ ମାଡ଼ି ସୁବିଧାରେ ବସନ୍ତୁ। ପିଠି ସିଧା ରଖି ଶାନ୍ତ ଭାବରେ ଶ୍ୱାସ ନିଅନ୍ତୁ।

🐍

6. Bhujangasana

ଭୁଜଙ୍ଗାସନ

  • Lie on the stomach.
  • Place the palms near the shoulders.
  • Raise the chest gently.
  • Keep the movement comfortable.
  • Return slowly.

ପେଟ ତଳକୁ କରି ଶୋଇ ହାତକୁ କାନ୍ଧ ପାଖରେ ରଖନ୍ତୁ। ଛାତିକୁ ଧୀରେ ଉଠାଇ ପୁଣି ତଳକୁ ଆସନ୍ତୁ।

🌙

7. Shavasana

ଶବାସନ

  • Lie comfortably on your back.
  • Keep arms and legs relaxed.
  • Close the eyes gently.
  • Breathe slowly.
  • Relax the whole body.

ପିଠି ଉପରେ ଶୋଇ ହାତ, ଗୋଡ଼ ଓ ସମଗ୍ର ଶରୀରକୁ ଶିଥିଳ କରନ୍ତୁ।

🕉️

8. Meditation

ଧ୍ୟାନ

  • Sit in a comfortable position.
  • Keep the body relaxed.
  • Close the eyes if comfortable.
  • Focus on natural breathing.
  • Practise quietly for a few minutes.

ସୁବିଧାରେ ବସି ଆଖି ବନ୍ଦ କରନ୍ତୁ। ସ୍ୱାଭାବିକ ଶ୍ୱାସ ଉପରେ ଧ୍ୟାନ ଦେଇ କିଛି ସମୟ ଶାନ୍ତ ଭାବରେ ରୁହନ୍ତୁ।

Yoga Safety Rules | ଯୋଗର ସୁରକ୍ଷା ନିୟମ

  • Practise under teacher or adult supervision.
  • Do not force the body into any position.
  • Breathe normally and comfortably.
  • Practise on a clean, level surface.
  • Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort.
  • Ask a qualified adult before practising if you have an injury or health concern.
