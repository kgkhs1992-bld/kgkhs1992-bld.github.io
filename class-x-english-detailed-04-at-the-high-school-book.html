CHAPTER 04Mahatma Gandhi

📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. What kind of student did Gandhi consider himself to be?
Answer: He was a conscientious student who cared about his studies and character.
Q2. What did Gandhi receive in the fifth and sixth standards?
Answer: He obtained prizes and scholarships.
Q3. What activities were made compulsory for upper-standard boys?
Answer: Gymnastics and cricket were made compulsory.
Q4. Why did Gandhi dislike gymnastics and cricket?
Answer: He was shy and also wanted to serve his father at home.
Q5. What did Gandhi later realize about physical training?
Answer: He realized that physical training is important along with mental training.
Q6. What did Gandhi think about his teachers?
Answer: He valued their guidance and was concerned about maintaining his character before them.
Q7. Why was Sanskrit important in the lesson?
Answer: The Sanskrit teacher emphasized its importance as the language of Gandhi’s cultural tradition.
Q8. Why was Gandhi attracted to Persian?
Answer: He found Persian easier and was influenced by the Persian teacher.
Q9. What did Gandhi later realize about handwriting?
Answer: He realized that good handwriting is an important part of education.
Q10. What does the lesson reveal about Gandhi’s school life?
Answer: It reveals his studies, teachers, discipline, physical training, language learning and self-reflection.
