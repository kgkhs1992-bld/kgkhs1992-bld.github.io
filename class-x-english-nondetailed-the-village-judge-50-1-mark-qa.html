The Village Judge

Class X English • Non-Detailed Text • 50 One-Mark Questions & Answers

← Back to Class X English
1. Who wrote 'The Village Judge'?
Answer: Prem Chand.
2. Who were the two close friends?
Answer: Jumman Sheikh and Algu Chaudhuri.
3. Who was Jumman's old relative?
Answer: His aunt.
4. What did Jumman's aunt possess?
Answer: A small piece of property.
5. To whom did the aunt transfer her property?
Answer: Jumman.
6. How was the aunt treated before transferring her property?
Answer: With kindness and respect.
7. How was she treated afterward?
Answer: Harshly and rudely.
8. Who was Jumman's wife?
Answer: Kariman.
9. What did the aunt ask Jumman for?
Answer: A monthly allowance.
10. What did Jumman say about money?
Answer: He said money did not grow on trees.
11. What did the aunt decide to call?
Answer: A panchayat.
12. Whom did the aunt finally approach for support?
Answer: Algu Chaudhuri.
13. Why did Algu hesitate to help?
Answer: Jumman was his close friend.
14. What question from the aunt troubled Algu?
Answer: Whether he would avoid the truth because of friendship.
15. Where did the first panchayat meet?
Answer: Under a tree.
16. Who was chosen as the chief judge in the first panchayat?
Answer: Algu Chaudhuri.
17. Why did the aunt choose Algu?
Answer: She trusted him to be fair.
18. What did Jumman think about Algu's selection?
Answer: He believed the decision would favor him.
19. What principle did the aunt state about a panch?
Answer: The panch should have no friend or enemy.
20. What did Algu decide about the dispute?
Answer: The aunt should receive a monthly allowance or get her property back.
21. How did Jumman react to Algu's judgement?
Answer: He was angry and felt betrayed.
22. What happened to the friendship after the first judgement?
Answer: It broke.
23. What did Jumman want after the friendship ended?
Answer: Revenge.
24. What did Algu own besides his property?
Answer: A pair of bullocks.
25. Where had Algu bought the bullocks?
Answer: At the Batesar fair.
26. What happened to one bullock?
Answer: It died.
27. Who bought the remaining bullock?
Answer: Samjhoo Sahu.
28. What was Samjhoo Sahu's occupation?
Answer: He drove an ekka and carried goods to town.
29. What did Sahu promise about payment?
Answer: He promised to pay the price within a month.
30. How did Sahu treat the bullock?
Answer: He overworked it and neglected its food and water.
31. What happened to the bullock?
Answer: It died from overwork.
32. Why did Sahu refuse to pay Algu?
Answer: He claimed Algu had sold him a dying bullock.
33. What did the villagers advise?
Answer: They advised taking the matter to a panchayat.
34. Whom did Sahu choose as his panch?
Answer: Jumman Sheikh.
35. What did Jumman think when he became judge?
Answer: That he must speak the truth.
36. What was Jumman's final judgement?
Answer: Sahu should pay the full price of the bullock.
37. Why did the bullock die according to Jumman?
Answer: It was overworked and not properly fed.
38. How did Algu react to Jumman's judgement?
Answer: He was happy.
39. What did Algu call the panch after the judgement?
Answer: The voice of God.
40. What did Jumman do after giving judgement?
Answer: He embraced Algu.
41. What did Jumman realize about the panch?
Answer: That God speaks through the panch.
42. Who is the central character besides Algu?
Answer: Jumman Sheikh.
43. What does 'panch' mean in the story?
Answer: A member acting as a village judge.
44. What value is emphasized by the story?
Answer: Justice and truth.
45. What did Algu do when friendship conflicted with justice?
Answer: He chose justice.
46. What did Jumman do when he had the chance to take revenge?
Answer: He chose truth.
47. What caused the second dispute?
Answer: The sale and death of Algu's bullock.
48. What was the amount Sahu had to pay?
Answer: The full price of the bullock.
49. Who is the author of the story?
Answer: Prem Chand.
