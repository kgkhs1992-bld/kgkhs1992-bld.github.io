📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. What is air pollution?
Answer: It is the alteration of the normal composition of air by harmful substances or gases.
Q2. What are major sources of air pollution?
Answer: Industrial activities and automobile exhaust are major sources discussed in the lesson.
Q3. How does air pollution affect the human body?
Answer: It can cause eye irritation, scratchy throats and respiratory illnesses and is associated with serious diseases.
Q4. Which gases are discussed in relation to global warming?
Answer: Carbon dioxide, methane and nitrous oxide are discussed.
Q5. What is global warming in the context of the lesson?
Answer: It is the warming of the atmosphere associated with gases that trap radiation from the sun.
Q6. What is acid rain?
Answer: It is rain or other precipitation containing sulphur and nitrogen oxides and other chemicals.
Q7. How does acid rain affect water bodies?
Answer: It can make lakes and rivers acidic and harm aquatic plants and animals.
Q8. How does acid rain affect forests?
Answer: It can remove nutrients from soil and contribute to the death of forests.
Q9. What are the three approaches to control air pollution?
Answer: Preventive, dispersal and collection measures.
Q10. What is a collection measure?
Answer: It uses equipment to trap pollutants before they escape into the atmosphere.
Q11. Why can pollution become an international problem?
Answer: Pollutants can be carried by wind from one country to another.
Q12. How can automobile pollution be reduced?
Answer: The lesson mentions redesigned engines and devices such as catalytic converters.
Q13. What is the role of legislation?
Answer: Laws and standards are used to prevent and control air pollution.
Q14. Why is air pollution called a hidden menace?
Answer: Its harmful effects may be widespread and serious even though pollution is not always immediately visible.
Q15. What does the lesson suggest about controlling pollution?
Answer: Control requires research, technology, laws, standards and expenditure.
