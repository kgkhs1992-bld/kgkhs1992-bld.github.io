📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. What does the poet think of all things?
Answer: The poet thinks that all things are bright, beautiful, wise and wonderful.
Q2. Who has made all these things?
Answer: The Lord God has made all these things.
Q3. What are the little things mentioned in the poem?
Answer: The little flowers and little birds are the little things mentioned.
Q4. How does the poet describe the beauty of flowers?
Answer: He describes their glowing colours as beautiful.
Q5. How does the poet describe the little birds?
Answer: He describes their singing and tiny wings.
Q6. How is the mountain described?
Answer: It is described as a purple-headed mountain.
Q7. Why does the mountain appear purple-headed?
Answer: The rays of the setting sun make its top appear bluish red or purple.
Q8. What brightens up the sky?
Answer: The sunset and the morning brighten up the sky.
Q9. Which two seasons are mentioned?
Answer: Winter and summer are mentioned.
Q10. Why does the poet consider the summer sun pleasant?
Answer: The poet describes the summer sun as pleasant as part of the beauty of creation.
Q11. How are the meadows useful for us?
Answer: They are places where we can run and play.
Q12. Where do the rushes grow?
Answer: They grow by the water.
Q13. Why do people gather rushes?
Answer: They gather them for useful purposes such as making mats and baskets.
Q14. Why has God given us eyes?
Answer: God has given us eyes to see the beautiful things around us.
Q15. What should our lips tell?
Answer: Our lips should tell how great God Almighty is.
Q16. How does the poet describe God?
Answer: He describes God as Almighty and as the creator who made all things well.
