📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. What does the poem imagine flowers to be?
Answer: It imagines flowers as children attending a school.
Q2. Where is the flower-school?
Answer: It is imagined as being underground.
Q3. What happens while the flowers are at school?
Answer: They remain inside and attend their lessons under the teacher’s discipline.
Q4. What brings the flower children their holidays?
Answer: The coming of the rain brings their holidays.
Q5. What do the flowers do during their holidays?
Answer: They come out and dance on the grass in colourful dresses.
Q6. What natural elements are personified?
Answer: Flowers, clouds, wind, sky and stars are presented with human qualities.
Q7. Why is the school imagined underground?
Answer: It is an imaginative picture of flowers growing beneath the soil before appearing above ground.
Q8. What is the effect of the monsoon on the flowers?
Answer: It releases them from the imagined school and allows them to appear in the open.
Q9. Who wrote The Flower-School?
Answer: Rabindranath Tagore wrote it.
Q10. What is the central idea?
Answer: The poem uses imagination and personification to present nature as a playful school and the flowers as children.
