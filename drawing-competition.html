Drawing Competition

ଚିତ୍ରାଙ୍କନ ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତା

Show Your Creativity

Drawing competitions help students develop creativity, imagination, observation and artistic skills.

ଚିତ୍ରାଙ୍କନ ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତା ଛାତ୍ରଛାତ୍ରୀମାନଙ୍କର ସୃଜନଶୀଳତା, କଳ୍ପନାଶକ୍ତି, ପର୍ଯ୍ୟବେକ୍ଷଣ ଓ କଳାଦକ୍ଷତା ବିକଶିତ କରେ।

1. Competition Topics

ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତାର ବିଷୟ

  • Nature and Environment
  • My School
  • Save Trees and Save Earth
  • Birds and Animals
  • Our National Festivals
  • Clean and Green Village

2. Required Materials

ଆବଶ୍ୟକ ସାମଗ୍ରୀ

  • Drawing paper
  • Pencil and eraser
  • Sharpener
  • Colour pencils or crayons
  • Oil pastels or water colours
  • Scale and drawing board

3. Competition Rules

ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତାର ନିୟମ

  • Complete the drawing within the given time.
  • Use your own ideas.
  • Keep the drawing neat and clean.
  • Do not copy another student's work.
  • Write your name and class clearly.
  • Follow the teacher's instructions.

4. Judging Criteria

ମୂଲ୍ୟାୟନର ମାନଦଣ୍ଡ

  • Creativity and originality
  • Relevance to the topic
  • Neatness and presentation
  • Use of colours
  • Composition and balance
  • Overall artistic expression

5. Student Preparation

ଛାତ୍ରଛାତ୍ରୀଙ୍କ ପ୍ରସ୍ତୁତି

  • Practise drawing regularly.
  • Observe nature and surroundings.
  • Learn to use colours properly.
  • Prepare necessary materials.
  • Keep your work original.
  • Draw with confidence.

6. Awards and Display

ପୁରସ୍କାର ଓ ପ୍ରଦର୍ଶନୀ

  • Best drawings may receive prizes.
  • Selected artwork may be displayed.
  • Students may receive certificates.
  • Outstanding work may be shown in the school gallery.

Notice | ସୂଚନା

The date, time, venue and topic of each drawing competition will be announced by the school.

ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ଚିତ୍ରାଙ୍କନ ପ୍ରତିଯୋଗିତାର ତାରିଖ, ସମୟ, ସ୍ଥାନ ଓ ବିଷୟ ବିଦ୍ୟାଳୟ ପକ୍ଷରୁ ପରେ ଘୋଷଣା କରାଯିବ।
