Chapter 02 – The Swimmer Who Does Not Need Her Legs!

KGK HIGH SCHOOL BELDANDIA

Book Q & A • Class IX English • Jasmine

Textbook Questions & Answers

1. When did Janaki get the idea of swimming the English Channel?
Answer: She got the idea as a child.
2. What did she plan to do then?
Answer: She planned to try to swim the English Channel.
3. Who said, “I was able to swim 10 hours at a stretch in the swimming pool”?
Answer: Janaki.
4. Where did she go with the help of Thimaiah Academy of Adventure?
Answer: She went to the coastal town of Malpe.
5. What did she do at Malpe?
Answer: She trained in the Arabian Sea and swam a minimum of 20 kilometres a day.
6. Who contributed the money needed for the attempt?
Answer: The government, some private agencies and several friends contributed money.
7. Who was Stella Streeter?
Answer: She was a famous Channel coach who trained Janaki for about three weeks.
8. What does “Janaki's Maritime Express” mean?
Answer: It was the name given to the relay team in honour of Janaki.
9. What were the conditions set before a swimmer could attempt the Channel?
Answer: The swimmer had to be able to stay in water for at least ten continuous hours and do marathon swimming in the high seas.
10. Why were the Channel Swimming Association officials shocked?
Answer: It was the first time in their experience that a physically handicapped person was trying to swim the English Channel.
11. Why was Janaki made part of a relay team?
Answer: The officials did not have a special boat to accompany her for a solo attempt.
12. What were the Channel waters like?
Answer: They were very cold and salty, with waves, seaweeds and jellyfish.
13. What problems did Janaki face during the swim?
Answer: The cold water, waves moving her legs, salty water, seaweeds and jellyfish made the swim difficult.
14. What were Janaki's parents feeling while she crossed the Channel?
Answer: They were very anxious and worried about their daughter.
15. What does “Paraplegic Olympics” mean in the context of the lesson?
Answer: It refers to the sports event for paraplegic athletes that Janaki planned to train for.
16. What did Janaki want to prove?
Answer: She wanted to prove that being handicapped was no bar to success.
17. What qualities did Janaki say help a person overcome obstacles?
Answer: Hard work, determination, courage and self-reliance.
18. What did Janaki say about success?
Answer: She said there was really no short-cut to success.

Textbook Language & Writing Practice

The chapter includes negative prefixes, phrases such as at a stretch, leave for, in spite of, a lot of and keep a sharp eye, the use of could and be able to, and a formal letter asking for information about a swimming course.
