⭐ Additional Questions & Answers

Extra practice questions prepared from the chapter content.

Q1. What does Lencho’s second letter reveal about his character?
Answer: It shows that his faith in God remains strong, but he does not understand who actually helped him.
Q2. Why did the postmaster decide to help Lencho?
Answer: He wanted to preserve Lencho’s faith in God.
Q3. What is the irony at the end of the story?
Answer: The people who helped Lencho are suspected by Lencho of being dishonest.
Q4. What does the expression “an act of charity” mean?
Answer: It means helping someone in need without expecting a return.
Q5. Why is the title appropriate?
Answer: The story centres on Lencho’s letter to God and the response he receives through human kindness.
Q6. What does the story show about faith?
Answer: It shows how deeply Lencho depends on his faith during a crisis.
Q7. What role does the postmaster play?
Answer: He becomes the link through which Lencho receives help.
Q8. How does nature affect the story?
Answer: Rain first gives Lencho hope, while hail destroys his crop and creates the central crisis.
Q9. What is the main conflict?
Answer: The main conflict is between Lencho’s need after crop failure and the loss caused by the storm.
Q10. What is one important theme of the story?
Answer: Faith and human kindness are major themes.
