Worksheet

Class VIII English • Jasmine • Unit 4: Environment • The Cherry Tree

Instructions: Read the lesson carefully and answer in your own words. This worksheet follows the characters, events, vocabulary, grammar and ideas presented in the textbook.

1. Comprehension

  1. Where did Rakesh live and with whom?
  2. Why did Rakesh’s parents send him to Mussoorie?
  3. How much did Rakesh pay for the bunch of cherries?
  4. Where did Grandfather tell Rakesh to plant the seed?
  5. What happened to the young cherry tree when a goat entered the garden?
  6. What happened to the tree when a woman cutting grass struck it with her scythe?
  7. Who were some of the visitors to the cherry tree?
  8. Why was the tree special to Rakesh and Grandfather?

2. Put the Events in the Correct Order

Number these events from 1 to 8.

  1. Rakesh planted the cherry seed.
  2. The goat ate the leaves of the young tree.
  3. Rakesh bought a bunch of cherries.
  4. The tree produced its first pale pink blossom.
  5. Rakesh discovered that the seed had grown into a tiny cherry tree.
  6. A woman cutting grass cut the tree in two.
  7. Grandfather advised Rakesh to plant the seed.
  8. The tree grew taller than Rakesh and later Grandfather.
