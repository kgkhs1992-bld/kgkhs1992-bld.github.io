📝 Class IX Geometry — Chapter 06

CONSTRUCTION — Study Material

This chapter explains geometric constructions using given lengths, angles, medians, diagonals and related conditions.

6.1 Construction of Triangles

The textbook gives several constructions of a triangle from specified sides, angles and combinations of side lengths.

  • One side, an adjacent angle and the sum of the other two sides.
  • One side, an adjacent angle and the difference between the other two sides.
  • Two sides and the included angle.
  • Two sides and an angle opposite one of them.
  • One side, an adjacent angle and a median to another side.

6.2 Important Construction Idea

A construction is carried out by drawing the required base/side, constructing the required angle, using arcs with the given lengths, and locating the required intersection point.

Always use the given measurements exactly and label every constructed point.

6.3 Special Triangle Constructions

The chapter includes constructions involving specified side differences, medians and related conditions. The construction is justified by showing that the resulting lengths and angles satisfy the given data.

6.4 Construction of Quadrilaterals

The textbook includes quadrilateral constructions from:

  • four sides and one angle
  • four sides and one diagonal
  • three sides and two diagonals
  • two adjacent sides and three angles

A useful method is to draw a diagonal first and construct the two triangles on its sides.

Equal-Area Constructions

The chapter includes constructions such as drawing a triangle equal in area to a given quadrilateral, drawing an isosceles triangle equal in area to a given triangle, drawing a right-angled triangle equal in area to a given triangle, and drawing a rectangle equal in area to a given triangle.

Triangle area = ½ × base × corresponding height

6.7 Division of a Line Segment

A given line segment can be divided into any number of congruent parts. For division into 5 equal parts, an auxiliary ray is drawn from one endpoint, five equal intervals are marked on it, and parallel lines are used to transfer the division to the given segment.

⭐ Quick Revision

  • Use ruler and compass carefully.
  • Draw auxiliary lines when required.
  • Use arcs to transfer given lengths.
  • Use parallel lines for equal division.
  • Check the final figure against every given condition.
