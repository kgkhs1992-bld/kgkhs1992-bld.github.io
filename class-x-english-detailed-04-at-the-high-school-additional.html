⭐ Additional Questions & Answers

Extra practice questions prepared from the chapter content.

Q1. Why is the lesson called autobiographical?
Answer: Gandhi narrates experiences from his own school life.
Q2. What does Gandhi’s concern about character show?
Answer: It shows that he attached importance to honesty and personal conduct.
Q3. How can physical education support academic education?
Answer: It develops the body and complements mental development.
Q4. What does the Sanskrit teacher’s advice show?
Answer: It shows the teacher’s concern that Gandhi should value Sanskrit and his cultural learning.
Q5. What does Gandhi’s later reflection on handwriting teach?
Answer: It shows that he was willing to recognize and correct weaknesses.
Q6. How does the lesson show the influence of teachers?
Answer: Different teachers influenced Gandhi’s studies, interests and attitudes.
Q7. What conflict appears between Sanskrit and Persian learning?
Answer: Gandhi feels attracted to Persian while the Sanskrit teacher urges him to continue Sanskrit.
Q8. Why is self-reflection important in the lesson?
Answer: Gandhi looks back at his choices and identifies lessons learned from them.
Q9. What can students learn from Gandhi’s school experiences?
Answer: They can learn the importance of discipline, study, physical training and good character.
Q10. What makes an autobiographical lesson different from a fictional story?
Answer: It presents the writer’s own remembered experiences rather than an invented narrative.
