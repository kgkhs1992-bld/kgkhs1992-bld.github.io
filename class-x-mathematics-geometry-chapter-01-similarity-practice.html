Practice set: Extra questions for Chapter 01, arranged from basic ratio work to theorem-based and proof-based problems. These are additional practice questions, not a replacement for the textbook exercise.

Section A – Basic Similarity & Ratios

1 Two similar triangles have corresponding sides 6 cm and 9 cm. Find their scale factor.
2 If two corresponding sides of similar triangles are 8 cm and 12 cm, find the ratio of their areas.
3 Two similar triangles have areas 49 cm² and 81 cm². Find the ratio of corresponding sides.
4 ΔABC ~ ΔPQR. AB = 7 cm, PQ = 14 cm and BC = 9 cm. Find QR.
5 ΔABC ~ ΔDEF. AB = 5 cm, BC = 7 cm, CA = 9 cm and DE = 10 cm. Find EF and FD.
6 The perimeters of two similar triangles are 24 cm and 36 cm. If a side of the first is 8 cm, find the corresponding side of the second.
7 Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio 3:5. If the smaller triangle has area 54 cm², find the area of the larger triangle.
8 If the areas of two similar triangles are in the ratio 25:49, find the ratio of their corresponding altitudes.
9 Two similar triangles have corresponding medians 12 cm and 18 cm. If a corresponding side of the smaller is 14 cm, find the side of the larger.
10 A triangle has sides 6 cm, 8 cm and 10 cm. Another triangle has sides 9 cm, 12 cm and 15 cm. Are they similar? Give the criterion.

Section B – Basic Proportionality Theorem

11 In ΔABC, D lies on AB and E lies on AC. If DE ∥ BC, AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 5 cm, find EC.
12 In ΔPQR, X lies on PQ and Y on PR. If XY ∥ QR, PX = 3 cm, XQ = 5 cm and PY = 4.5 cm, find YR.
13 In ΔABC, D divides AB in the ratio 2:3 and E divides AC in the same ratio. Prove DE ∥ BC.
14 In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC. If AD:DB = 3:5 and AC = 24 cm, find AE and EC.
15 In ΔABC, D and E lie on AB and AC respectively. AD = 5, DB = 3, AE = 10 and EC = 6. Determine whether DE ∥ BC.
16 In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC and AD/AB = 2/5. If AC = 20 cm, find AE and EC.
17 A line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally. State and prove this theorem.
18 State and prove the converse of the Basic Proportionality Theorem.

Section C – Similarity Criteria

19 Two triangles have angles 50°, 60°, 70° and 50°, 60°, 70°. Prove that they are similar.
20 In two triangles, two pairs of corresponding sides are proportional and the included angles are equal. Name the similarity criterion.
21 The sides of two triangles are 4, 6, 8 and 6, 9, 12. Determine whether they are similar.
22 ΔABC and ΔDEF have AB/DE = AC/DF = 5/7 and ∠A = ∠D. Prove ΔABC ~ ΔDEF.
23 Explain the difference between AA, SAS and SSS similarity criteria.
24 If ΔABC ~ ΔPQR and AB/PQ = 4/7, write the ratios BC/QR and CA/RP.
25 If ΔABC ~ ΔDEF and AB = 12 cm, DE = 18 cm, BC = 16 cm, find EF.

Section D – Angle Bisector Theorem & Applications

26 In ΔABC, AD bisects ∠A. If AB = 8 cm, AC = 12 cm and BC = 15 cm, find BD and DC.
27 In ΔPQR, PS bisects ∠P. If PQ = 6 cm, PR = 9 cm and QR = 10 cm, find QS and SR.
28 State the Angle Bisector Theorem.
29 Prove the Angle Bisector Theorem using similar triangles.
30 Prove the converse of the Angle Bisector Theorem.

Section E – Right Triangle Similarity

31 In a right triangle, the altitude from the right angle to the hypotenuse divides it into segments 9 cm and 16 cm. Find the altitude.
32 In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is 25 cm and one segment created by the altitude is 9 cm. Find the other segment.
33 If AD ⟂ BC in right ΔABC and BD = 4 cm, DC = 9 cm, find AD.
34 Using similarity, prove AB² = AD × AC for a right triangle with altitude AD to hypotenuse BC.
35 Using similarity, prove BC² = CD × CA.
36 Using similarity, prove AD² = BD × DC.

Section F – Proof & Higher Order Practice

37 Prove that corresponding altitudes of similar triangles are proportional to their corresponding sides.
38 Prove that corresponding medians of similar triangles are proportional to corresponding sides.
39 Prove that corresponding angle bisectors of similar triangles are proportional to corresponding sides.
40 Prove that the ratio of areas of similar triangles equals the square of the ratio of corresponding sides.
41 Two similar triangles have equal areas. Prove that they are congruent.
42 Two similar triangles have equal perimeters. Prove that they are congruent.
43 In ΔABC, D is the midpoint of AB and E is the midpoint of AC. Prove DE ∥ BC and DE = BC/2.
44 Prove that the line joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle divides the triangle into a smaller triangle similar to the original triangle.
45 If two triangles are similar in the ratio 2:3, find the ratio of their areas, altitudes, medians and angle bisectors.

Section G – Challenge Problems

46 In ΔABC, DE ∥ BC. If area(ΔADE) : area(ΔABC) = 4:25 and AB = 15 cm, find AD.
47 Two similar triangles have areas 144 cm² and 225 cm². If a side of the smaller triangle is 16 cm, find the corresponding side of the larger triangle.
48 A line parallel to BC cuts AB and AC at D and E. If AD:DB = 2:3 and the area of ΔADE is 32 cm², find the area of trapezium DBCE.
49 In two similar triangles, corresponding angle bisectors are 8 cm and 12 cm. If the area of the smaller triangle is 80 cm², find the area of the larger triangle.
50 In a right triangle, the altitude to the hypotenuse divides it into segments 5 cm and 20 cm. Find the hypotenuse, altitude and the two legs.
Answer: Hypotenuse = 25 cm; altitude = 10 cm; legs = 5√5 cm and 10√5 cm.
