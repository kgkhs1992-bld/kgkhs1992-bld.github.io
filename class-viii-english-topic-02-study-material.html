Subject: English

🎯 Learning Objectives

After studying this topic, students should read the lesson carefully, understand the poem, learn the important words, and practise the textbook questions and answers.

📖 How to Study This Topic

Step 1 – Read the Textbook

Read the complete lesson from the textbook PDF carefully.

Step 2 – Read the Poem

Read the poem “A Concrete Example” slowly and clearly.

Step 3 – Learn New Words

Write difficult words from the lesson in your notebook and learn their meanings.

Step 4 – Practise the Questions

Read and write the textbook questions and answers.

Step 5 – Revise

Revise the poem, meanings, questions and answers before the examination.
