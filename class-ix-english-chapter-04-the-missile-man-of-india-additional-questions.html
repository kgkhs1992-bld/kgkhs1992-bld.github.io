Additional Questions

One-mark practice questions.

  1. Give Kalam's date of birth.
    Answer: A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.
  2. Name Kalam's birthplace.
    Answer: Rameswaram.
  3. Name his father.
    Answer: Jainulabdeen.
  4. Name his mother.
    Answer: Ashiamma.
  5. What did his father own?
    Answer: A boat/ferry.
  6. What religious position did his father hold?
    Answer: Imam of a local mosque.
  7. How many brothers and sisters are mentioned?
    Answer: Four brothers and one sister are mentioned.
  8. Why did Kalam sell newspapers?
    Answer: To supplement family income.
  9. Name his science teacher.
    Answer: Sivasubramania Iyer.
  10. Where did his elementary education begin?
    Answer: Ramanathapuram.
  11. Name the higher secondary school he attended.
    Answer: Schwartz Higher Secondary School.
  12. Where did he study physics?
    Answer: Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.
  13. What was his dream?
    Answer: To become a pilot.
  14. Who helped him pay the engineering fee?
    Answer: His elder sister Asim Zohra.
  15. How did she help?
    Answer: She mortgaged her gold ornaments.
  16. When did he complete aerospace engineering?
    Answer: 1960.
  17. Name the first organisation where he trained.
    Answer: Hindustan Aeronautics Limited.
  18. What does HAL stand for?
    Answer: Hindustan Aeronautics Limited.
  19. Name the organisation he joined after HAL.
    Answer: DRDO.
  20. When did he move to ISRO?
    Answer: 1969.
  21. What does ISRO stand for?
    Answer: Indian Space Research Organisation.
  22. What was his role in SLV-III?
    Answer: Project Director of SLV-III.
  23. Name the Rohini satellite programme result.
    Answer: The successful launch of the Rohini satellite into orbit.
  24. How many fruitful years did the textbook say he spent in ISRO?
    Answer: Nineteen years.
  25. What missile programme did he lead?
    Answer: Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme.
  26. Name one missile developed under the programme.
    Answer: Prithvi.
  27. Name all four missiles mentioned.
    Answer: Prithvi, Trishul, Akash and Nag.
  28. What was Pokhran-II?
    Answer: Nuclear tests at Pokhran-II.
  29. What desert site is associated with the tests?
    Answer: The Thar Desert.
  30. Name one civilian award Kalam received.
    Answer: Padma Bhushan.
  31. Which is India's highest civilian award?
    Answer: Bharat Ratna.
  32. When did Kalam become President?
    Answer: 2002.
  33. What number President was he?
    Answer: 11th.
  34. What popular title was he given?
    Answer: The People's President.
  35. Where did he deliver his final lecture?
    Answer: Shillong.
  36. On what date did he die?
    Answer: 27 July 2015.
  37. What was the cause of death stated in the chapter?
    Answer: A massive cardiac arrest, as stated in the textbook.
  38. Name one quality of Kalam.
    Answer: Hard work and determination.
  39. Name one contribution to space research.
    Answer: His work on India's space programme.
  40. Name one contribution to missile development.
    Answer: His missile-development work.
  41. What does 'distinguished' mean?
    Answer: Successful and admired.
  42. What does 'prestigious' mean?
    Answer: Respected and admired.
  43. What does 'compassionate' mean?
    Answer: Kindhearted or sympathetic.
  44. What does 'aerospace' relate to?
    Answer: Aircraft and spacecraft engineering.
  45. What does 'launch' mean?
    Answer: To start or send something into operation/space.
  46. What does 'satellite' mean?
    Answer: An object placed in space for a purpose such as communication.
  47. What does 'fruitful' mean?
    Answer: Producing useful results.
  48. What does 'selfless' mean?
    Answer: Serving without selfishness.
  49. What lesson can students learn from Kalam?
    Answer: Learning, persistence and service.
