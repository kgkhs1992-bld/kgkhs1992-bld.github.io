⚠️ Drill Safety Rules

  • Practise under the teacher’s supervision.
  • Warm up before physical activity.
  • Wear comfortable sports clothes.
  • Drink water when needed.
  • Do not force any movement.
  • Stop if you feel pain or dizziness.
