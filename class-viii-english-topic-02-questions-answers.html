Unit 1: Wit and Wisdom – The Wit That Won Hearts

📘 Class VIII English: Important textbook questions, answers, grammar exercises, sound words, compound words, if-clause exercises and listening questions.

📖 1. Discuss in Pairs – Page 2

Q1. What is wit?
Answer: Wit is the ability to think quickly and cleverly and to say or do something intelligent.
ଓଡ଼ିଆ: ବୁଦ୍ଧିଚାତୁରୀ ହେଉଛି ଶୀଘ୍ର ଓ ଚତୁର ଭାବରେ ଚିନ୍ତା କରି ବୁଦ୍ଧିମାନ କଥା କହିବା କିମ୍ବା କାମ କରିବାର କ୍ଷମତା।
Q2. Who is a witty person?
Answer: A witty person is one who uses intelligence and humour to solve problems or make others laugh.
Q3. Can wit help us in difficult situations?
Answer: Yes, wit can help us to find quick and clever solutions in difficult situations.
Q4. Why should we use our wit wisely?
Answer: We should use our wit wisely so that we can solve problems without hurting others.

📖 2. Let Us Discuss – Page 7

Q1. The scholar was very humble.
Answer: False. The scholar was arrogant.
Q2. Rama accepted the challenge of the scholar.
Answer: True.
Q3. Rama showed a bundle of silk clothes.
Answer: False. Rama showed a bundle of sticks.
Q4. The king became angry with Rama.
Answer: False. The king laughed and appreciated Rama’s wit.
Q5. The scholar was proud of his knowledge.
Answer: True.

📖 3. Let Us Think and Reflect – Page 7

Q1. Why did the scholar visit the king’s court?
Answer: The scholar visited the king’s court because he wanted to prove that he was more intelligent than everyone else.
Q2. Why were the court scholars afraid?
Answer: The court scholars were afraid because they feared the king’s anger if they failed to answer the scholar’s questions.
Q3. How did Rama accept the challenge?
Answer: Rama confidently accepted the challenge and decided to use his wit to defeat the scholar.
Q4. What made the king laugh?
Answer: Rama’s clever trick and witty answer made the king laugh.

📖 4. Questions and Answers – Page 8

Q1. What did Rama bring to the court?
Answer: Rama brought a bundle of sticks tied with silk.
Q2. What did Rama ask the scholar to do?
Answer: Rama asked the scholar to identify the bundle and explain its importance.
Q3. How did Rama prove his intelligence?
Answer: Rama proved his intelligence by using a clever trick and giving a witty answer.
Q4. What lesson do we learn from the story?
Answer: We learn that intelligence, presence of mind and humility are more valuable than pride.

📖 5. Answer the Following Questions

Q1. Who was the scholar?
Answer: The scholar was a learned but arrogant person who wanted to prove his superiority.
Q2. Why did the scholar come to the king’s court?
Answer: He came to the king’s court to challenge the scholars and prove that he was the most intelligent person.
Q3. Why did the court scholars remain silent?
Answer: They remained silent because they were afraid of the scholar and the king’s anger.
Q4. What did Rama do?
Answer: Rama accepted the challenge and used his wit to teach the arrogant scholar a lesson.
Q5. How did the king react?
Answer: The king laughed heartily and appreciated Rama’s intelligence.
Q6. What is the moral of the story?
Answer: The moral is that wit and presence of mind can defeat pride and arrogance.

📚 10. If-Clause Identification

“If you work hard, you will succeed.”
Answer: If you work hard.
“If I were rich, I would help the poor.”
Answer: If I were rich.
“If you had listened carefully, you would have solved the puzzle.”
Answer: If you had listened carefully.

📚 11. If-Clause Matching

  1. If I had a magic wand → I would give myself wings.
  2. If it rains tomorrow → We will stay indoors and play board games.
  3. If you had listened carefully → You would have solved the puzzle.
  4. If you finish your homework → We will go to the theatre.
  5. If I were invisible → I would sneak into the secret room.
  6. If the alarm had rung → The boys would have woken up on time.
