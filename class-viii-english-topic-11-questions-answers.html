Questions & Answers

Class 8 • Jasmine English • Chapter 11
Environment: Harvest Hymn

📘 Text-Based Questions & Answers

1. Whom do the men praise in the first stanza?
Answer: The men praise Surya, the Sun God. They thank him for the sunlight and bounty that helped their sowing and nurtured their corn.
2. Why do the men offer songs, garlands and the gold of their fields and fruit?
Answer: They offer these as a tribute to Surya and as an expression of gratitude for the blessings that helped their crops grow.
3. What does “the gold of our fields” refer to?
Answer: It refers to the golden-coloured crops or harvest produced in their fields.
4. Who is Varuna in the poem?
Answer: Varuna is praised as the giver of rain and dewfall. The men thank him because rain and dew nourish the fields and help the grain grow.
5. What do the men offer to Varuna?
Answer: They offer their thanks, garlands and the newly gathered, ripe wealth of their valleys as tribute.
6. What is meant by “our furrows”?
Answer: “Our furrows” refers to the long narrow trenches or lines in the soil made for cultivation by a plough.
7. Whom do the women praise in the third stanza?
Answer: The women praise Prithvi, Mother Earth. They regard her as the mother who feeds them and gives birth to their crops and riches.
8. Why is Prithvi called the mother?
Answer: Prithvi is called the mother because the earth provides food and gives birth to crops and other riches on which people depend.
9. What do the women offer to Prithvi?
Answer: They offer their love, garlands and the gifts of the abundant harvest received from the earth.
10. What do all the voices praise in the final stanza?
Answer: All the voices praise Brahma, described as the eternal Father and the Lord of the Universe and of all being.
11. What does Brahma represent in the final stanza?
Answer: Brahma represents the supreme creative power. The speakers see this power in the seed, the scythe, their hands, hearts and homes.
12. What do the people offer to Brahma?
Answer: They offer their lives and labour as a tribute and ask for his help, counsel and care.
13. Name the four divine figures praised in the poem.
Answer: The four are Surya, Varuna, Prithvi and Brahma.
14. What is the central idea of “Harvest Hymn”?
Answer: The poem expresses gratitude for the natural and divine forces connected with a successful harvest. It celebrates sunlight, rain, the earth and the creative power that sustains human life.
15. How do music and offerings appear in the poem?
Answer: The people express gratitude through songs, garlands, harvest offerings and musical instruments such as cymbals, flute, pipe and drum.

🌾 Short Answer Questions

1. What helped the sowing prosper?
The bounty of Surya, especially his life-giving radiance, helped the sowing prosper.
2. What helped the grain grow?
Varuna’s rain and dewfall helped the grain grow.
3. Who brings love and garlands as tribute?
The women bring love and garlands as tribute to Prithvi.
4. What do the people ask Brahma to grant them?
They ask for succour, counsel and care.

🧠 Think & Reflect

1. How does the poem show gratitude towards nature?
It shows gratitude by thanking the Sun for light, Varuna for rain and dew, and Earth for food and crops. The people offer songs, garlands, harvest and their labour as tributes.
2. How are the men's and women's voices different?
The men praise Surya and Varuna, whose sunlight, rain and dew support cultivation. The women praise Prithvi, the Earth who feeds them and gives birth to their riches. In the final stanza, all voices unite.
3. How does the poem connect human work with nature?
The poem presents sowing, growing grain and harvesting as activities that depend on sunlight, rain and the earth. Human labour is therefore shown together with natural blessings.
4. What feeling dominates the poem?
A strong feeling of gratitude and praise dominates the poem. The speakers repeatedly offer thanks and tribute for the blessings connected with the harvest.
