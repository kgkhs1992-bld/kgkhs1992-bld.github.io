Part A — 5-Mark Questions & Answers

1. Probability କ'ଣ? Experiment ଓ Observation ବୁଝାଅ।

ଉତ୍ତର:
Probability କୌଣସି event ଘଟିବାର chanceକୁ ମାପେ।
Experiment ହେଉଛି ଏପରି ଏକ ପ୍ରକ୍ରିୟା ଯାହାର result/observation ମିଳେ।
ଯଥା coin toss ଓ die throw।
Coin tossରେ Head କିମ୍ବା Tail observation ହୋଇପାରେ।
ଏହି observation ଆଧାରରେ eventର probability ଅଧ୍ୟୟନ କରାଯାଏ।

2. Empirical probability କ'ଣ?

ଉତ୍ତର:
କୌଣସି event E, ମୋଟ nଟି trialରେ m ଥର ଘଟିଲେ empirical probability = m/n।
ଏହା actual experimentର observation ଉପରେ ନିର୍ଭର କରେ।
ଉଦାହରଣ: 20ଥର coin tossରେ Head 12ଥର ଆସିଲେ P(H) empirical =12/20=3/5।
Trial ସଂଖ୍ୟା ବଢ଼ିଲେ experimental value theoretical probability ନିକଟକୁ ଯାଇପାରେ।
ଏହା experimental probabilityର ମୂଳ ଧାରଣା।

3. ଏକ fair die ଏକଥର throw କଲେ 4 ଆସିବାର probability ନିର୍ଣ୍ଣୟ କର।

ଉତ୍ତର:
ଏକ fair dieର possible outcomes ହେଉଛି 1,2,3,4,5,6।
ମୋଟ equally likely outcomes =6।
4 ଆସିବା ପାଇଁ favourable outcome କେବଳ 1ଟି।
ତେଣୁ P(4)=favourable outcomes/total outcomes =1/6।
ଅର୍ଥାତ fair dieରେ 4 ଆସିବାର probability 1/6।

4. Theoretical probabilityର formula ଓ probabilityର range ଲେଖ।

ଉତ୍ତର:
Equally likely outcomes ଥିଲେ theoretical probabilityର formula: P(E)=favourable outcomes/total number of equally likely outcomes।
ଏଠାରେ favourable outcomes event Eକୁ ସଫଳ କରୁଥିବା outcomes।
Total outcomes ସମସ୍ତ equally likely possible outcomes।
କୌଣସି event E ପାଇଁ 0≤P(E)≤1।
P(E)=0 ହେଲେ impossible event ଏବଂ P(E)=1 ହେଲେ certain event।

5. Complementary events ବୁଝାଅ ଏବଂ ସମ୍ବନ୍ଧିତ formula ଲେଖ।

ଉତ୍ତର:
Event E ଘଟିବା ନୁହେଁ ବୋଲି eventକୁ Eର complementary event କୁହାଯାଏ।
ଯଦି Eର probability P(E), ତେବେ complementର probability P(not E)=1−P(E)।
ତେଣୁ P(E)+P(not E)=1।
ଉଦାହରଣ: P(E)=0.4 ହେଲେ P(not E)=1−0.4=0.6।
ଦୁଇଟି complementary eventsର probabilityର ଯୋଗ ସବୁବେଳେ 1।
