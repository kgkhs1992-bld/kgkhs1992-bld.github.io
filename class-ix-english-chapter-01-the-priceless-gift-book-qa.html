Chapter 01 – The Priceless Gift

Class IX English • Jasmine • Book Questions & Answers

Textbook Questions & Answers

1. What did the writer experience in the restaurant?
Answer: He had lunch in a crowded vegetarian restaurant and noticed a young girl looking at him.
2. What did he notice there?
Answer: He noticed a very young girl with large, sad-looking eyes.
3. What was the writer’s first impression of the girl?
Answer: He thought she looked sad, anxious and poverty-stricken.
4. What made the writer surprised?
Answer: Maggie asked the cashier whether he was an Indian.
5. Why did he enquire from the cashier?
Answer: Her interest in him aroused his curiosity.
6. What information did he get from the cashier?
Answer: She came on Saturdays, on pay day, and perhaps did not earn much.
7. Where did the writer go on the next Saturday?
Answer: He went to the same restaurant.
8. Why did he arrange the chair opposite hers?
Answer: He wanted to meet her and start a conversation.
9. What query did the girl make about the writer?
Answer: She asked whether he was an Indian.
10. What impression did the writer have about the family?
Answer: He understood that they were poor and worried about Franky.
11. How was the writer treated at Maggie’s house? Who welcomed him?
Answer: Maggie welcomed him and introduced him to Mrs. Bethy.
12. What was Mrs. Bethy doing? Why was she unwilling to shake hands?
Answer: She was making cakes; her hands were covered with flour.
13. Why did Maggie usually visit the restaurant on Saturdays?
Answer: She came on pay day and apparently did not earn much.
14. What did Mrs. Bethy believe about the ring?
Answer: She believed it could show a distant person and what he was doing.
15. What made the author tell a lie about the ring?
Answer: Maggie asked him to tell her mother that Franky was alive so that her mother might recover.
16. Did the lie benefit Mrs. Bethy?
Answer: Yes. She soon recovered after hearing that Franky was alive and well.
17. Why did the writer feel guilty?
Answer: He had told Mrs. Bethy that Franky was alive, but later learned Franky had been killed.
18. What was Maggie’s gift to the writer?
Answer: A shilling to buy flowers for Franky’s grave.
19. Why was it a priceless gift?
Answer: It was earned through toil and represented love and sacrifice.
20. Did Maggie love her brother dearly?
Answer: Yes. She worried about him and sacrificed her hard-earned money for flowers on his grave.
21. Why did the writer choose the corner table?
Answer: He saw chairs and tables pushed into a corner and chose the corner table.
22. How did the writer start a conversation with Maggie?
Answer: He wished her a good afternoon and made brief remarks until conversation began.
23. How could he guess she was poverty-stricken?
Answer: The waiter said she came on pay day and perhaps did not earn much; her figure was sad and anxious.
24. Which would the author talk about after meeting her: meeting again, work, or family?
Answer: All the above.
25. What impression did the writer get about the girl?
Answer: He was moved by her poverty and anxiety and became concerned about her.
26. What impression did the girl have of India?
Answer: She was curious about India and asked whether it was safe and whether there were snakes and tigers.
27. Why did Maggie say “Will it be too much of a lie”?
Answer: She wanted Mr. Gupta to tell her mother that Franky was alive although it was not true.
28. Why did the writer feel ashamed when returning to India?
Answer: He knew Franky had been killed after he had told Mrs. Bethy that Franky was alive.
29. Why was the shilling an invaluable offering?
Answer: It was earned with great toil and given out of love and sacrifice for her brother.
