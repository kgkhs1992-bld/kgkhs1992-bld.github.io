50 questions with answers
Questions are based on the supplied Class IX English textbook story. Extracts are kept short and used as study cues.
Extract 1: “the sun was setting”
Q1. When did Sukumar enter the village?
Answer: He entered as the sun was setting.
Extract 2: “about eleven”
Q2. How old was Sukumar?
Answer: He was about eleven years old.
Extract 3: “the majestic Himalayas”
Q3. What mountain range is mentioned?
Answer: The Himalayas are mentioned.
Extract 4: “looking for a place”
Q4. Why was Sukumar in the village?
Answer: He was looking for a place to spend the night.
Extract 5: “his own mother”
Q5. What did the girl's call remind Sukumar of?
Answer: It reminded him of his dead mother.
Extract 6: “took out his flute”
Q6. Why did Sukumar play?
Answer: Memories of his mother led him to play the flute.
Extract 7: “village headman”
Q7. Who was the middle-aged woman?
Answer: She was the wife of the village headman.
Extract 8: “My son”
Q8. How did the woman address Sukumar?
Answer: She addressed him affectionately as her son.
Extract 9: “a stranger here”
Q9. How did Sukumar describe himself?
Answer: He said he was a stranger in the village.
Extract 10: “come with me”
Q10. What did the woman invite Sukumar to do?
Answer: She invited him to come home with her.
Extract 11: “your sister”
Q11. Whom did the woman say was calling her?
Answer: She said her daughter was calling her.
Extract 12: “your tune”
Q12. What did the woman feel about Sukumar's flute?
Answer: She felt the tune was calling her.
Extract 13: “lost his own mother”
Q13. What had happened to Sukumar at age five?
Answer: He had lost his mother.
Extract 14: “lived within his heart”
Q14. Where did Sukumar believe his mother still lived?
Answer: He believed she lived in his heart.
Extract 15: “a gift from his father”
Q15. Where did Sukumar's flute come from?
Answer: His father had given it to him.
Extract 16: “the house of the Gods”
Q16. What did his father say about his mother?
Answer: He said she lived in the house of the Gods.
Extract 17: “when I play the flute”
Q17. When could the father feel close to the mother?
Answer: He could feel close to her when he played the flute.
Extract 18: “make me a flute”
Q18. What did Sukumar ask his father to do?
Answer: He asked his father to make him a flute.
Extract 19: “taught him”
Q19. What did the father teach Sukumar?
Answer: He taught him how to play the flute.
Extract 20: “Sayapatri”
Q20. Who became close to Sukumar?
Answer: Sayapatri became close to him like a sister.
Extract 21: “his sister”
Q21. How did Sukumar regard Sayapatri?
Answer: He regarded her as his sister.
Extract 22: “did not love him”
Q22. Why did Sukumar become unhappy?
Answer: He felt that Sayapatri's mother did not love him.
Extract 23: “left their house”
Q23. What did Sukumar eventually do?
Answer: He left their house.
Extract 24: “a shrine on a mountain”
Q24. Where did Sukumar go after leaving?
Answer: He went to live in a shrine on a mountain.
Extract 25: “a dream”
Q25. What led Sukumar back?
Answer: He had a dream about being called back.
Extract 26: “very sick”
Q26. What did Sukumar find when he returned?
Answer: He found Sayapatri very sick.
Extract 27: “a very high fever”
Q27. What illness did Sayapatri have?
Answer: She had a very high fever.
Extract 28: “in a coma”
Q28. What was Sayapatri's condition?
Answer: She was in a coma.
Extract 29: “revive her”
Q29. What did Sayapatri's mother ask Sukumar to do?
Answer: She asked him to do something to revive her.
Extract 30: “played it very quietly”
Q30. What did Sukumar do?
Answer: He played his flute quietly.
Extract 31: “her eyelids began to flutter”
Q31. What happened as the tune sounded?
Answer: Sayapatri began to regain consciousness.
Extract 32: “her eyes opened”
Q32. What happened next?
Answer: Sayapatri opened her eyes.
Extract 33: “my brother”
Q33. What did Sayapatri call Sukumar?
Answer: She called him her brother.
Extract 34: “you've returned”
Q34. What did Sayapatri realize?
Answer: She realized Sukumar had returned.
Extract 35: “tears in his eyes”
Q35. How did Sayapatri's father react?

Extract 36: “my own dear son”
Q36. What did the father finally call Sukumar?
Answer: He called him his own dear son.
Extract 37: “never let you go”
Q37. What did the father promise?

Extract 38: “tears of joy”
Q38. How did the mother react at the end?
Answer: She shed tears of joy.
Extract 39: “looked at each other”
Q39. What did Sayapatri and Sukumar do at the end?
Answer: They looked at each other and smiled.
Extract 40: “the flute”
Q40. Why is the flute central to the story?
Answer: It connects Sukumar with his mother and later helps reunite him with Sayapatri.
Extract 41: “the magical effect”
Q41. Why is the flute called magical?
Answer: Its music is associated with healing, memory and reunion.
Extract 42: “mother's memory”
Q42. What does the flute symbolize for Sukumar?
Answer: It symbolizes his continuing bond with his mother.
Extract 43: “family”
Q43. What does the ending restore?

Extract 44: “the dream”
Q44. What function does the dream serve?

Extract 45: “the mountain shrine”
Q45. Why did Sukumar stay there?

Extract 46: “the village woman”
Q46. What did the woman first offer Sukumar?
Answer: She offered him shelter and affection.
Extract 47: “the father”
Q47. Who made Sukumar's first flute?
Answer: His father made it.
Extract 48: “Sayapatri's recovery”
Q48. What caused Sayapatri to recover?
Answer: She responded to Sukumar's flute music.
Extract 49: “the happy ending”
Q49. How does the story end?
Answer: The family accepts Sukumar and Sayapatri and Sukumar remain reunited.
