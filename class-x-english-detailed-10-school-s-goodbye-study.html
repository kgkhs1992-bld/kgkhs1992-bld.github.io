📖 Chapter at a Glance

Author / Poet: Lord Eustace Percy
Chapter: School's Goodbye
Textbook page: 99

The lesson is a farewell message to students who are leaving school. It reminds them that education does not end with school and that success requires hard work and continued learning. It advises students to choose their profession carefully, maintain good health and character, use leisure wisely, avoid harmful habits and remain loyal to their responsibilities and ideals.

About the Author

Lord Eustace Percy was a former Minister of Education in Britain. The textbook presents “School’s Goodbye” as a message to school children about life after school.

Choosing a Career

Students are advised to choose a trade or profession carefully. If an unsuitable job has to be taken temporarily, they should continue looking for work that offers a satisfying future and seek advice when uncertain.

Good Health

The lesson stresses cleanliness, fresh air, regular habits and suitable recreation. It warns against habits that damage health and efficiency.

Character

Character is presented as a priceless possession. The advice includes truthfulness, consideration for others, fairness, kindness, helpfulness and courage to stand for what is good.

Use of Leisure

Students are advised not to waste leisure in idleness. Hobbies, good books and healthy companionships can influence life positively.

Citizenship and Success

The concluding message connects a sound mind, sound body, good character, courteous manners and loyalty to national duties with becoming a good citizen and living a successful life.

Important Glossary

welfare — well-being; ambitious — strongly wishing to succeed; occupation — job or profession; temperate — moderate; untarnished — not damaged or stained; provision — preparation; courteous — polite; loyalty — faithful support.

Textbook Focus

1. Why does education not end with school?
2. How should students choose a profession?
3. How can good health be maintained?
4. Why is character called priceless?
5. How should leisure hours be used?
6. What qualities are connected with success?

Revision Points

Remember the five major areas: continuing education, choosing a profession, good health, sound character and useful leisure.
