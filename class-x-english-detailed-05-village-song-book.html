📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. Who are the two speakers in the poem?
Answer: The mother and her daughter are the two speakers.
Q2. Where does the honey child go?
Answer: She wants to go to the wild forest.
Q3. Why does she leave the mother and grieve the lover?
Answer: She is irresistibly attracted to nature and the call of the fairy folk.
Q4. How does the child describe the wild forest?
Answer: She describes champa blossoms, koil-haunted river-isles and shining lotus lilies.
Q5. What does “the world full of pleasure” mean?
Answer: It refers to worldly joys such as bridal songs, cradle songs and wedding preparations.
Q6. What is common about bridal songs and cradle songs?
Answer: The poet says both have cadences of sorrow.
Q7. How does the poet describe happiness and sorrow?
Answer: Happiness is linked with the laughter of the sun, while sorrow is associated with the wind of death.
Q8. How are forest notes compared with bridal and cradle songs?
Answer: The daughter says the forest notes are far sweeter.
Q9. What does the mother want to tell her daughter?
Answer: She wants her daughter to remain for the expected marriage and enjoy family life.
Q10. What does the girl find irresistible?
Answer: She finds the call of the fairy folk and the beauty of nature irresistible.
Q11. Who wrote Village Song?
Answer: Sarojini Naidu wrote it.
Q12. What is the daughter attracted to?
Answer: She is attracted to the forest, flowers, birds, river-isles and natural music.
Q13. What are bridal robes?
Answer: They are wedding clothes.
Q14. What are cradle songs?
Answer: They are lullabies.
Q15. What is the main contrast in the poem?
Answer: The poem contrasts worldly/domestic pleasures with the daughter’s attraction to nature.
