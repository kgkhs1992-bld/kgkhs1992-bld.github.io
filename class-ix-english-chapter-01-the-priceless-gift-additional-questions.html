Chapter 01 – The Priceless Gift

Class IX English • Jasmine • 50 One-Mark Additional Questions

50 One-Mark Additional Questions

Each question carries 1 mark.

  1. Who is the narrator?
    Answer: Mr. Gupta.
  2. Where did he go for lunch?
    Answer: A vegetarian restaurant.
  3. When did he first notice Maggie?
    Answer: At lunch time.
  4. How did Maggie’s eyes look?
    Answer: Large and sad.
  5. What did Maggie ask the cashier?
    Answer: Whether the gentleman was an Indian.
  6. Which day did Maggie come to the restaurant?
    Answer: Saturday.
  7. Why did the waiter think she might be poor?
    Answer: She came on pay day and perhaps did not earn much.
  8. What was Maggie’s mother’s name?
    Answer: Mrs. Bethy.
  9. What was Maggie’s brother’s name?
    Answer: Franky.
  10. Where was Franky a soldier?
    Answer: India.
  11. Why was Mrs. Bethy worried?
    Answer: She had no letter from Franky.
  12. What was being repaired?
    Answer: The village bridge.
  13. What key did Maggie use?
    Answer: A thin latch key.
  14. What was Mrs. Bethy making?
    Answer: Cakes.
  15. Why could she not shake hands immediately?
    Answer: Her hands were covered with flour.
  16. What did Mrs. Bethy show Mr. Gupta?
    Answer: Franky’s photograph.
  17. What did Franky send?
    Answer: A ring.
  18. What power did Mrs. Bethy believe the ring had?
    Answer: It could show a distant person and what he was doing.
  19. How long passed without news?
    Answer: Several months.
  20. What happened to Mrs. Bethy?
    Answer: She became seriously ill.
  21. What did Maggie ask Mr. Gupta to say?
    Answer: That Franky was alive and well.
  22. What happened after he said it?
    Answer: Mrs. Bethy recovered.
  23. Where was Franky killed?
    Answer: In fighting on the frontier.
  24. What did Maggie wear when saying goodbye?
    Answer: Black.
  25. Where was Franky buried?
    Answer: Near the frontier.
  26. What did Maggie give Mr. Gupta?
    Answer: A shilling.
  27. What was he asked to buy?
    Answer: Flowers.
  28. Where were they to be placed?
    Answer: On Franky’s grave.
  29. Why did he accept the shilling?
    Answer: He did not want to deprive Maggie of the joy of her sacrifice.
  30. What did he promise to do?
    Answer: Buy flowers and put them on Franky’s grave.
  31. What did he wipe from his eyes?
    Answer: A tear.
  32. What does aroused mean?
    Answer: Awakened or stirred.
  33. What does persisted mean?
    Answer: Continued to exist.
  34. What does festivity mean?
    Answer: Celebration.
  35. What does aggravated mean?
    Answer: Worsened.
  36. What does gratitude mean?
    Answer: The feeling of being grateful.
  37. What does profusion mean?
    Answer: Abundance or plenty.
  38. What does foregoing mean?
    Answer: Sacrificing.
  39. What is a grave?
    Answer: A place where a dead person is buried.
  40. What is a shilling?
    Answer: A unit of money.
  41. What grammar topic is introduced in section G?
    Answer: Active and Passive Voice.
  42. What is used with the past participle in passive voice?
    Answer: The relevant tense of be.
  43. What is the Simple Present passive form?
    Answer: is/am/are + past participle.
  44. What is the Simple Past passive form?
    Answer: was/were + past participle.
  45. What is the Future passive form?
    Answer: shall/will + be + past participle.
  46. What does Maggie’s shilling represent?
    Answer: Love and sacrifice for her brother.
