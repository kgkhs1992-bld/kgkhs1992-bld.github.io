📖 Chapter at a Glance

Author / Poet: Dr. Reeta S. Mani
Chapter: From the Formalin Jar
Textbook page: 88

The poem presents a voice from a brain preserved in a formalin jar. The poem uses this striking image to draw attention to road accidents and road safety. Its introductory discussion highlights the serious human cost of road injuries and identifies risk factors such as speed, alcohol, seat belts, helmets, visibility and distracted driving.

Lead-in and Context

The textbook introduces the poem through the problem of road accidents. It stresses awareness of safety rules and identifies several major risk factors.

The Speaker

The speaker is presented imaginatively as Vicky’s brain, now preserved in a formalin jar. The unusual viewpoint makes the reader think about what road accidents can do to a human life.

Central Message

The poem links the preserved brain with road safety. It encourages readers to think about responsible road behaviour and the consequences of unsafe driving.

Road-safety Ideas

Important risks highlighted in the lesson include excessive speed, alcohol, failure to use a seat belt or helmet, poor visibility, distraction such as mobile-phone use or music, tiredness and unsafe roads.

Important Glossary

formalin — preservative solution; disdain — feeling of contempt; accident — unexpected harmful event; violation — breaking a rule; visibility — ability to see or be seen; distracted — unable to give full attention; risk — possibility of harm.

Textbook Focus

1. Who is the speaker in the poem?
2. Why is the brain kept in a formalin jar?
3. What does the poem make us think about?
4. What are the major road-safety risk factors introduced before the poem?

Language and Study

The chapter also supports work with vocabulary, word formation and participle adjectives. The textbook uses the topic to connect language learning with a real-life safety issue.

Revision Points

Remember the formalin-jar image, the road-safety context, major risk factors and the poem’s warning about the consequences of unsafe behaviour.
