Geometry – Chapter 05

ପରିମିତି — Mensuration

🔄 Quick Revision

🔙 ପଛକୁ – Class X Mathematics

📐 Formula Sheet

Circle: C=2πr, A=πr²

Arc: L=(θ/360)×2πr

Sector: A=(θ/360)πr², P=2r+L

Rectangle: A=lb, P=2(l+b)

Square: A=a², P=4a

Triangle: A=1/2bh

Trapezium: A=1/2(a+b)h

Cylinder: V=πr²h, CSA=2πrh

Cone: l=√(r²+h²), V=1/3πr²h, CSA=πrl

Sphere: SA=4πr², V=4/3πr³

Hemisphere: CSA=2πr², TSA=3πr², V=2/3πr³

📌 1. ପରିଧି ଓ ବୃତ୍ତ

ବ୍ୟାସ d = 2r; ପରିଧି C = 2πr = πd; ବୃତ୍ତର କ୍ଷେତ୍ରଫଳ A = πr²।

📌 2. Arc Length

θ° ର arc length: L = (θ/360) × 2πr = θπr/180। θ=360° ହେଲେ ସମଗ୍ର ପରିଧି, θ=180° ହେଲେ ଅର୍ଦ୍ଧ ପରିଧି।

📌 3. Sector

Sector ହେଉଛି ଦୁଇଟି radius ଓ ଗୋଟିଏ arc ଦ୍ୱାରା ସୀମିତ ଅଂଶ। Area = (θ/360)πr²। Perimeter = 2r + L।

📌 4. Minor ଓ Major Sector

Minor sector ର କେନ୍ଦ୍ରୀୟ କୋଣ 180° ରୁ କମ୍; major sector ର କୋଣ 180° ରୁ ଅଧିକ। Major angle = 360° − minor angle।

📌 5. ଅର୍ଦ୍ଧବୃତ୍ତ

Semicircle area = 1/2 πr²। ଏହାର perimeter = πr + 2r, କାରଣ arc ସହ diameter ମଧ୍ୟ boundary ର ଅଂଶ।

📌 6. ଆୟତ ଓ ବର୍ଗ

Rectangle: Area=lb, Perimeter=2(l+b)। Square: Area=a², Perimeter=4a।

📌 7. ତ୍ରିଭୁଜ ଓ ଟ୍ରାପିଜିୟମ୍

Triangle area = 1/2 bh। Trapezium area = 1/2(a+b)h, ଯେଉଁଠାରେ a,b ସମାନ୍ତର ପାର୍ଶ୍ୱ।

📌 8. ଘନ ଓ ଆୟତଘନ

Cube: Volume=a³, TSA=6a²। Cuboid: Volume=lbh, TSA=2(lb+bh+hl)।

📌 9. Cylinder

Volume=πr²h; Curved Surface Area=2πrh; Total Surface Area=2πr(h+r)।

📌 10. Cone

Slant height l=√(r²+h²); Volume=1/3πr²h; CSA=πrl; TSA=πr(l+r)।

📌 11. Sphere

Surface area=4πr²; Volume=4/3πr³।

📌 12. Hemisphere

Curved surface area=2πr²; Total surface area=3πr²; Volume=2/3πr³।

📌 13. Units

Length: cm/m; Area: cm²/m²; Volume: cm³/m³। 1 m=100 cm, 1 m²=10,000 cm², 1 m³=1,000,000 cm³।

📌 14. Numerical ଲେଖିବାର ନିୟମ

Given → Required → Formula → Substitution → Calculation → Final answer with unit।

🧮 10 Quick Worked Examples

ଉଦାହରଣ 1: r=14 cm, 90° arc

L=(90/360)×2×22/7×14 = 22 cm।

ଉଦାହରଣ 2: r=14 cm, 90° sector

Area=(90/360)×22/7×14² = 154 cm²।

ଉଦାହରଣ 3: r=14 cm, 90° sector perimeter

L=22 cm; P=2(14)+22=50 cm।

ଉଦାହରଣ 4: d=28 cm circle

r=14 cm; C=2×22/7×14=88 cm।

ଉଦାହରଣ 5: r=7 cm circle area

A=22/7×7²=154 cm²।

ଉଦାହରଣ 6: d=14 cm semicircle area

r=7 cm; A=1/2×22/7×49=77 cm²।

ଉଦାହରଣ 7: rectangle 12 cm × 8 cm

Area=12×8=96 cm²; perimeter=2(20)=40 cm।

ଉଦାହରଣ 8: square side 9 cm

Area=81 cm²; perimeter=36 cm।

ଉଦାହରଣ 9: cylinder r=7 cm, h=10 cm

V=22/7×49×10=1540 cm³; CSA=2×22/7×7×10=440 cm²।

ଉଦାହରଣ 10: cone r=6 cm, h=8 cm

l=√(36+64)=10 cm; V=1/3×π×36×8=96π cm³।

✅ Exam-Day Checklist

  1. Given data ଠିକ୍ ଭାବେ ଲେଖନ୍ତୁ।
  2. Diameter ଦିଆଥିଲେ r=d/2 କରନ୍ତୁ।
  3. θ/360 factor ଥିବା arc/sector formula ମନେ ରଖନ୍ତୁ।
  4. Area ରେ square unit, volume ରେ cubic unit ଦିଅନ୍ତୁ।
  5. 5-mark numerical ରେ formula ଓ working steps ଛାଡ଼ନ୍ତୁ ନାହିଁ।
  6. Final answer କୁ box କରି unit ସହ ଲେଖନ୍ତୁ।
