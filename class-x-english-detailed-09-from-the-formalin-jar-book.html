📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. What is the striking image in the poem?
Answer: A brain is imagined as being preserved in a formalin jar.
Q2. What real-life issue does the lesson focus on?
Answer: It focuses on road accidents and road safety.
Q3. Why is the formalin jar image effective?
Answer: It presents the consequences of an accident through an unusual and memorable viewpoint.
Q4. What risk factors are discussed in the lesson’s road-safety context?
Answer: Speed, alcohol, failure to use safety equipment, poor visibility and distraction are among the risks discussed.
Q5. Why are helmets important?
Answer: They reduce the risk of serious head injury in road accidents.
Q6. Why are seat belts important?
Answer: They provide restraint and reduce the risk of injury in a collision.
Q7. How can distraction cause danger?
Answer: It reduces the driver’s attention to the road and surrounding traffic.
Q8. What is the significance of the title?
Answer: The title draws attention to the preserved brain and connects it with the consequences of road accidents.
Q9. What is the main purpose of the poem?
Answer: It makes readers think seriously about road safety and the human consequences of unsafe behaviour.
Q10. What does formalin mean?
Answer: Formalin is a preservative solution used to preserve biological specimens.
