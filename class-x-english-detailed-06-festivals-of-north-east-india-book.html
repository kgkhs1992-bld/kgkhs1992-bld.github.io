📚 Book Questions & Answers

Textbook-aligned question-answer practice. The wording follows the textbook questions where they were retrievable from the uploaded book; answers are written in clear student-friendly form.

Q1. What is the main subject of the lesson?
Answer: It presents the festivals, traditions and cultural diversity of North-East India.
Q2. What are the three Bihus of Assam?
Answer: Rongali/Bohag Bihu, Kongali/Kaati Bihu and Bhogali/Magh Bihu.
Q3. What is Rongali or Bohag Bihu associated with?
Answer: It is associated with spring, the beginning of sowing and the Assamese New Year.
Q4. What is Kongali or Kaati Bihu associated with?
Answer: It is observed when crops are growing and the granaries are comparatively empty.
Q5. What is Bhogali or Magh Bihu associated with?
Answer: It is associated with the end of harvest and thanksgiving.
Q6. What is Wangala associated with?
Answer: It is a harvest-related festival of Meghalaya.
Q7. What does the Hornbill Festival showcase?
Answer: It showcases the cultural heritage and traditions of Nagaland.
Q8. What is Chapchar Kut associated with?
Answer: It is a spring festival of Mizoram.
Q9. Why are festivals important in the lesson?
Answer: They preserve cultural traditions and bring communities together.
Q10. What do traditional songs and dances contribute to festivals?
Answer: They express community identity, celebration and cultural heritage.
