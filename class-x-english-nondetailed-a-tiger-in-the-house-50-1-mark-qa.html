A Tiger in the House

Class X English • Non-Detailed Text • 50 One-Mark Questions & Answers

1. Who wrote 'A Tiger in the House'?
Answer: Ruskin Bond.
2. What is the name of the tiger?
Answer: Timothy.
3. Who discovered Timothy?
Answer: Grandfather.
4. Where did Grandfather discover Timothy?
Answer: During a hunting expedition.
5. What kind of animal was Timothy?
Answer: A tiger.
6. Where was Timothy brought up?
Answer: At Grandfather's home.
7. Who were Timothy's companions at home?
Answer: The writer and other members of the household.
8. How was Timothy brought up?
Answer: With proper care and a good diet.
9. How did Timothy behave when he was young?
Answer: He was friendly and playful.
10. What did Timothy become after about six months?
Answer: Less friendly and more fierce.
11. Why was Timothy sent to a zoo?
Answer: Because he became more dangerous.
12. Which zoo received Timothy?
Answer: The zoo at Lucknow.
13. Why did the zoo authorities accept Timothy?
Answer: He was well fed and fairly civilized.
14. How long after giving Timothy to the zoo did Grandfather visit?
Answer: About six months later.
15. Why did Grandfather visit the zoo?
Answer: To see Timothy.
16. What did Grandfather do at the tiger's cage?
Answer: He stroked and slapped the tiger affectionately.
17. How did the tiger behave toward Grandfather?
Answer: It licked his hand and appeared friendly.
18. What animal frightened the tiger in the cage?
Answer: A leopard.
19. What did Grandfather suggest about the leopard?
Answer: That Timothy should be moved away from it.
20. Whom did Grandfather want to see about transferring the tiger?
Answer: The Superintendent of the zoo.
21. Why could Grandfather not meet the Superintendent?
Answer: The Superintendent had gone home early.
22. Who was observing Grandfather near the cage?
Answer: A zoo keeper.
23. What did the keeper say about touching the tiger?
Answer: He said he had never been able to touch it.
24. What did the keeper say about the tiger's temper?
Answer: It was very bad-tempered.
25. Did Grandfather know that the tiger was no longer his?
Answer: Yes.
26. What did Grandfather say about the tiger being his?
Answer: He admitted it was no longer his.
27. What did the keeper finally tell Grandfather?
Answer: That Timothy had died two months earlier.
28. How had Timothy died?
Answer: Of pneumonia.
29. When had the new tiger been trapped?
Answer: The previous month.
30. Was the tiger in the cage really Timothy?
Answer: No.
31. How did Grandfather react when he heard Timothy had died?
Answer: He was shocked.
32. What was the new tiger doing to Grandfather?
Answer: It was licking his arm.
33. How did Grandfather leave the zoo?
Answer: He walked briskly out.
34. What did Grandfather say to the tiger before leaving?
Answer: Goodnight, Timothy.
35. What is the main surprise at the end of the story?
Answer: Grandfather had been affectionately treating a different tiger as Timothy.
36. Who is the author of the story?
Answer: Ruskin Bond.
37. What was Timothy's appearance connected with?
Answer: A tiger kept as a family pet.
38. What happened to Timothy when he became older?
Answer: He became less friendly and more dangerous.
39. What did the zoo do with Timothy?
Answer: It kept him in a cage.
40. What did the keeper think about the tiger Grandfather was visiting?
Answer: He thought it was a dangerous tiger.
41. What did Grandfather intend to discuss with the Superintendent?
Answer: Moving the tiger to another cage.
42. What was Grandfather's attitude toward the tiger?
Answer: Affectionate.
43. What does the ending create?
Answer: Irony and surprise.
44. Where was the zoo mentioned in the story?
Answer: Lucknow.
45. Who wrote the story in the textbook?
Answer: Ruskin Bond.
46. What did Timothy do when Grandfather came near him?
Answer: He responded affectionately.
47. What did the leopard do near the cage?
Answer: It rushed at the bars.
48. What did Timothy do when the leopard rushed at the bars?
Answer: He moved back to his corner.
49. What was the keeper's reaction to Grandfather's conversation?
Answer: He was surprised.
50. What did Grandfather realize at the end?
Answer: The tiger was not Timothy.
