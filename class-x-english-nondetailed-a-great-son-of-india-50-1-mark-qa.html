A Great Son of India

Class X English • Non-Detailed Text • 50 One-Mark Questions & Answers

← Back to Class X English
1. Who is the great son of India discussed in the lesson?
Answer: Swami Vivekananda.
2. Who delivered the speech about Vivekananda?
Answer: Dr. S. Radhakrishnan.
3. On what occasion was the speech delivered?
Answer: On Vivekananda's 92nd birth anniversary.
4. What competitions are mentioned at the beginning?
Answer: Recitation and speech competitions.
5. What did the students have the opportunity to read?
Answer: Writings of Swami Vivekananda.
6. What did Vivekananda's life and teachings prepare people for?
Answer: A new age of freedom.
7. What movement is Vivekananda associated with?
Answer: The Indian Renaissance.
8. Did Vivekananda claim to formulate a completely new system of thought?
Answer: No.
9. What did he interpret for India and the world?
Answer: India's religious consciousness.
10. Whose teachings influenced Vivekananda?
Answer: Sri Ramakrishna's.
11. What are two dominant features of the modern age mentioned by Radhakrishnan?
Answer: Science and democracy.
12. What must modern religious beliefs be supported by?
Answer: Reason and rational evidence.
13. What should religion do in a democracy?
Answer: Support democratic values and unity.
14. What kind of religion cannot be accepted in the modern age?
Answer: One that divides people or supports exploitation and war.
15. Why does religion appear to be in eclipse according to the speech?
Answer: Because some forms of religion appear unscientific and undemocratic.
16. What does transience mean in the lesson?
Answer: The temporary nature of worldly things.
17. What happens to all worldly things according to the speech?
Answer: They pass away with time.
18. What force is symbolized by birth and death?
Answer: Time.
19. What question about reality is raised in the speech?
Answer: Whether there is a Beyond underlying the changing world.
20. What do the Upanishads provide?
Answer: An explanation of the fundamental spiritual problem.
21. What are the Vedas according to Vivekananda?
Answer: Registers of spiritual experiences discovered by seers.
22. Why are the Vedas described as ever-expanding?
Answer: Because spiritual experience continues to unfold.
23. What is religion according to Vivekananda?
Answer: Yoga.
24. What does Yoga involve according to the lesson?
Answer: Personal change, adjustment and integration.
25. Which four Yogas are named?
Answer: Jnana, Raja, Bhakti and Karma Yoga.
26. What is the goal of these Yogas?
Answer: Spiritual realization.
27. What happens when particular creeds are placed above universal truths?
Answer: Intolerance may result.
28. What is secularism in the lesson?
Answer: Respecting all religions as sacred and allowing freedom of conscience.
29. What does freedom of conscience mean?
Answer: Each soul can choose its own path.
30. What does secularism require besides tolerance?
Answer: Understanding and loving other religions.
31. Whose experience is referred to in discussing religious harmony?
Answer: Sri Ramakrishna's.
32. What did Vivekananda criticize in religious practice?
Answer: Obscurantism, superstition and religious discrimination.
33. What is 'antar-jyoti'?
Answer: The inner divine light.
34. Where is the Divine according to the lesson?
Answer: In all human beings.
35. What is one ultimate test of true religion?
Answer: Recognition of truth.
36. What is another ultimate test of true religion?
Answer: Reconciliation with human beings.
37. What principle can overcome hatred?
Answer: Ahimsa or renunciation of hatred.
38. What did Vivekananda raise to the level of worship?
Answer: Work.
39. What did he encourage people to seek through service?
Answer: Salvation through service of God in man.
40. What kind of religion does the speech advocate?
Answer: A spiritual religion that transforms human society.
41. What is Ramrajya described as?
Answer: The Kingdom of God.
42. Who was Sri Ramakrishna?
Answer: Vivekananda's great spiritual master.
43. Who was the speaker?
Answer: Dr. S. Radhakrishnan.
44. What is one quality of Vivekananda's writings mentioned in the lesson?
Answer: They are strengthened by quotations from Indian scriptures.
45. What did Vivekananda's teachings aim to do?
Answer: Awaken spiritual life and promote harmony.
46. What should religion not support?
Answer: Division, privilege, exploitation and war.
47. What is the central message about human beings?
Answer: The Divine is present in every person.
48. What does the lesson emphasize about service?
Answer: Service to people can be treated as service to God.
49. What is the main subject of the lesson?
Answer: The life and teachings of Swami Vivekananda.
