📘 Class VIII English

Wit and Wisdom: Wisdom Paves the Way — Study Material

✍️ Writer Biography (ଲେଖକଙ୍କ ଜୀବନୀ)

English:

The play "Wisdom Paves the Way" is an adaptation of a famous traditional Indian folktale. While the original author is unknown, such folktales have been passed down orally from generation to generation. They are designed to teach valuable life lessons. This specific story highlights the power of keen observation, logical deduction, and how true wisdom is far more valuable than just seeing things on the surface.

Odia (ଓଡ଼ିଆରେ):

"ୱିଜଡମ୍ ପେଭସ୍ ଦ ୱେ" (ଜ୍ଞାନ ବାଟ କଢ଼ାଏ) ନାଟକଟି ଏକ ପ୍ରସିଦ୍ଧ ପାରମ୍ପରିକ ଭାରତୀୟ ଲୋକକଥାର ଏକ ରୂପାନ୍ତର ଅଟେ। ଯଦିଓ ଏହାର ମୂଳ ଲେଖକ ଅଜ୍ଞାତ, ଏହିପରି ଲୋକକଥାଗୁଡ଼ିକ ପିଢ଼ି ପରେ ପିଢ଼ି ମୌଖିକ ଭାବରେ ପ୍ରଚଳିତ ହୋଇଆସିଛି। ମୂଲ୍ୟବାନ ଜୀବନଶିକ୍ଷା ଦେବା ପାଇଁ ଏଗୁଡ଼ିକ ଉଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ। ଏହି ନିର୍ଦ୍ଦିଷ୍ଟ କାହାଣୀଟି ତୀକ୍ଷ୍ଣ ପର୍ଯ୍ୟବେକ୍ଷଣ, ତାର୍କିକ ସିଦ୍ଧାନ୍ତ ଏବଂ ପ୍ରକୃତ ଜ୍ଞାନର ଶକ୍ତି ଉପରେ ଆଲୋକପାତ କରେ।

English Meaning:

This section introduces the characters and the setting of the play. The story begins on a hot, dusty road leading to the city of Ujjain. Four young men are walking towards the city. They look tired from a long journey but are very determined to reach their destination.

🔹 Block 2: The Journey and Hopes

Original Text:

RAM DATT: (Wiping his brow) We have journeyed far from home, yet the prospect of finding work still eludes us.

HAR DATT: How long has it been since we left our village?

SHIV DATT: I believe it has been over a week- perhaps even longer.

RAM DATT: Ten days, to be precise. Every day feels longer than the last.

DEV DATT: Ten days are but a short span in the grand scheme of things. We may still have many more roads to wander before we find employment befitting all four of us.

RAM DATT: If only we could secure an audience with the King of Ujjain. I am confident he would recognise our talents and appoint us to his service.

SHIV DATT: I share your belief.

DEV DATT: Indeed, we ought to be united in this hope. That is why we press on towards Ujjain.

HAR DATT: (Pausing thoughtfully) Gaining an audience with the King is no simple task. We need to devise a strategy.

DEV DATT: Let us reach the city gates first. We can deliberate once we are closer.

ରାମ୍ ଦତ୍ତ: ଇଫ୍ ଓନଲି ୱି କୁଡ୍ ସିକ୍ୟୋର୍ ଆନ୍ ଅଡିଏନ୍ସ ୱିଥ୍ ଦ କିଙ୍ଗ୍ ଅଫ୍ ଉଜ୍ଜୈନ୍. ଆଇ ଆମ୍ କନଫିଡେଣ୍ଟ ହି ଉଡ୍ ରେକଗନାଇଜ୍ ଆୱାର୍ ଟ୍ୟାଲେଣ୍ଟସ୍ ଆଣ୍ଡ ଆପଏଣ୍ଟ୍ ଅସ୍ ଟୁ ହିଜ୍ ସର୍ଭିସ୍.

1. Summary (English)

The play "Wisdom Paves the Way" tells the story of four observant and highly intelligent young men—Ram Datt, Shiv Datt, Har Datt, and Dev Datt—who are journeying to the city of Ujjain in search of employment. They are hopeful that if they can secure an audience with the young King of Ujjain, he will recognize their talents and appoint them to his service. While walking along a dusty road, they notice fresh camel tracks. Through keen observation and logical deduction, they closely scrutinize the surroundings and silently figure out several specific physical conditions of the camel.

Shortly after, a distressed merchant approaches them, anxiously looking for his lost camel. The four men ask the merchant a series of highly specific questions: whether the camel is lame in one leg, blind in its right eye, possesses a short tail, and is suffering from stomach pain. Shocked by their 100% accurate description, the merchant assumes that since they know so much about the animal without claiming to have seen it, they must be the thieves who stole it. Ignoring their calm demeanor, the indignant merchant drags the four men to the King's court to face justice.

In the royal court, the King asks the young men to explain how they knew such intimate details about the missing camel. The men brilliantly break down their deductions: Ram Datt noticed one faint footprint among three clear ones, indicating a limp; Shiv Datt observed that leaves were only eaten on the left side of the road, meaning the camel was blind in its right eye; Har Datt saw blood droplets from mosquito bites, deducing the camel lacked a long tail to swat pests; and Dev Datt noticed faint hind footprints, indicating the camel was walking cautiously to ease belly pain. Marvelling at their flawless reasoning and profound wisdom, the King dismisses the merchant's baseless accusations. Impressed by their extraordinary intellect, the King appoints all four men as his royal advisers, proving that wisdom and keen observation ultimately pave the way to success and prosperity.

2. Summary (Odia) / ସାରାଂଶ (ଓଡ଼ିଆରେ)

"ୱିଜଡମ୍ ପେଭସ୍ ଦ ୱେ" (ଜ୍ଞାନ ବାଟ କଢ଼ାଏ) ନାଟକଟି ଚାରିଜଣ ବୁଦ୍ଧିମାନ ଯୁବକ—ରାମ ଦତ୍ତ, ଶିବ ଦତ୍ତ, ହରି ଦତ୍ତ ଏବଂ ଦେବ ଦତ୍ତଙ୍କ କାହାଣୀ ଉପରେ ଆଧାରିତ। ସେମାନେ ନିଜର ଦକ୍ଷତା ପ୍ରମାଣିତ କରି ରାଜାଙ୍କ ଦରବାରରେ କାମ ପାଇବା ଆଶାରେ ଉଜ୍ଜୟିନୀ ସହର ଅଭିମୁଖେ ଯାତ୍ରା କରୁଥିଲେ। ରାସ୍ତାରେ ଯିବା ସମୟରେ ସେମାନେ ଗୋଟିଏ ଓଟର ତାଜା ପାଦଚିହ୍ନ ଦେଖିଲେ ଏବଂ ନିଜର ତୀକ୍ଷ୍ଣ ପର୍ଯ୍ୟବେକ୍ଷଣ ଶକ୍ତି ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଓଟଟିର ଶାରୀରିକ ଅବସ୍ଥା ବିଷୟରେ ଅନେକ ତଥ୍ୟ ଅନୁମାନ କଲେ।

କିଛି ସମୟ ପରେ ସେମାନଙ୍କର ଭେଟ ଜଣେ ବ୍ୟସ୍ତବିବ୍ରତ ବ୍ୟବସାୟୀଙ୍କ ସହ ହେଲା, ଯିଏକି ତାଙ୍କର ହଜିଯାଇଥିବା ଓଟକୁ ଖୋଜୁଥିଲେ। ଯେତେବେଳେ ଯୁବକମାନେ ଓଟଟିକୁ ନଦେଖି ମଧ୍ୟ ତା'ର ସଠିକ୍ ବର୍ଣ୍ଣନା କଲେ (ଯେପରିକି ଗୋଟିଏ ଗୋଡ଼ରେ ଛୋଟା, ଡାହାଣ ଆଖିରେ ଅନ୍ଧ, ଛୋଟ ଲାଞ୍ଜ ଏବଂ ପେଟରେ ଯନ୍ତ୍ରଣା), ବ୍ୟବସାୟୀ ଜଣକ ଆଶ୍ଚର୍ଯ୍ୟ ହୋଇଗଲେ। ସେ ସନ୍ଦେହ କଲେ ଯେ ଏହି ଯୁବକମାନେ ହିଁ ତାଙ୍କ ଓଟକୁ ଚୋରି କରିଛନ୍ତି ଏବଂ ବିଚାର ପାଇଁ ସେମାନଙ୍କୁ ରାଜାଙ୍କ ଦରବାରକୁ ନେଇଗଲେ।

ଉଜ୍ଜୟିନୀର ରାଜାଙ୍କ ଦରବାରରେ, ରାଜା ଯୁବକମାନଙ୍କୁ ଏହାର କାରଣ ପଚାରିଲେ। ଯୁବକମାନେ ନିଜର ଚମତ୍କାର ତର୍କ ଏବଂ ପର୍ଯ୍ୟବେକ୍ଷଣର କାରଣ ବୁଝାଇଲେ: ପାଦଚିହ୍ନରୁ ଛୋଟା ହେବା, କେବଳ ବାମ ପଟର ପତ୍ର ଖାଇଥିବାରୁ ଡାହାଣ ଆଖିରେ ଅନ୍ଧ ହେବା, ମଶା କାମୁଡ଼ା ରକ୍ତରୁ ଛୋଟ ଲାଞ୍ଜ ଥିବା ଏବଂ ପଛ ଗୋଡ଼ର ଅସ୍ପଷ୍ଟ ଚିହ୍ନରୁ ପେଟ ଯନ୍ତ୍ରଣା ବିଷୟରେ ସେମାନେ କିପରି ଜାଣିପାରିଲେ ତାହା ଶୁଣି ରାଜା ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ପ୍ରଭାବିତ ହେଲେ। ରାଜା ବୁଝିପାରିଲେ ଯେ ଏମାନେ ଚୋର ନୁହଁନ୍ତି, ବରଂ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଜ୍ଞାନୀ ବ୍ୟକ୍ତି। ତେଣୁ ସେ ବ୍ୟବସାୟୀଙ୍କ ମିଥ୍ୟା ଅଭିଯୋଗକୁ ଖାରଜ କରିଦେଲେ ଏବଂ ସେହି ଚାରି ଯୁବକଙ୍କର ଅସାଧାରଣ ବୁଦ୍ଧିମତାରେ ସନ୍ତୁଷ୍ଟ ହୋଇ ସେମାନଙ୍କୁ ନିଜର ରାଜକୀୟ ଉପଦେଷ୍ଟା ଭାବରେ ନିଯୁକ୍ତ କଲେ। ଏହି କାହାଣୀ ପ୍ରମାଣ କରେ ଯେ ପ୍ରକୃତ ଜ୍ଞାନ ହିଁ ସଫଳତାର ମାର୍ଗ ଦେଖାଏ।
