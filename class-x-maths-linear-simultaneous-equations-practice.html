CHAPTER 01 — LINEAR SIMULTANEOUS EQUATIONS

⭐ Additional Practice

Practice these additional questions on pairs of linear simultaneous equations. Try to solve each question yourself before checking the answer.

Practice areas: Substitution, Elimination, Graphical Interpretation, No Solution, Infinitely Many Solutions and Applications.

📝 Practice Questions

Question 1

Solve the following pair of equations using the Substitution Method.

x + y = 9
x − y = 3
👁️ Show Answer
x = 6,   y = 3

Question 2

Solve using the Elimination Method.

2x + 3y = 16
2x − 3y = 4
👁️ Show Answer
x = 5,   y = 2

Question 3

Solve the following pair of equations.

3x + 2y = 18
3x − 2y = 6
👁️ Show Answer
x = 4,   y = 3

Question 4

Solve by substitution.

y = x + 2
2x + y = 11
👁️ Show Answer
x = 3,   y = 5

Question 5

Determine whether the following pair has a solution.

2x + 4y = 10
x + 2y = 7
👁️ Show Answer
There is no solution.

Question 6

Determine whether the following pair has one solution or infinitely many solutions.

3x + 6y = 12
x + 2y = 4
👁️ Show Answer
The two equations represent the same line.
Therefore, there are infinitely many solutions.

Question 7

Solve the following pair of equations.

5x + 2y = 19
3x − 2y = 5
👁️ Show Answer
x = 3,   y = 2

Question 8

The sum of two numbers is 20 and their difference is 4. Find the two numbers.

x + y = 20
x − y = 4
👁️ Show Answer
The two numbers are 12 and 8.

Question 9

The sum of two numbers is 30. One number is 6 more than the other. Find the numbers.

x + y = 30
x − y = 6
👁️ Show Answer
The numbers are 18 and 12.

Question 10

Solve the pair of equations and verify your answer.

4x + 3y = 18
2x − 3y = 0
👁️ Show Answer
x = 3,   y = 2

Verification:
4(3) + 3(2) = 18
2(3) − 3(2) = 0
