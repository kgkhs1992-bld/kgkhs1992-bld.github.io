The First Step

1-Mark Questions & Answers • 30 Questions

Q1. Who is Ammu?
Answer: A ten-year-old motherless girl.
Q2. Who is Ammu's father?
Answer: Chellappa.
Q3. Who is Ammu's brother?
Answer: Chinni.
Q4. What was Chellappa's job?
Answer: He was the watchman at a big house.
Q5. Why did Chellappa need money?
Answer: To send Chinni to school.
Q6. What did Ammu want to do?
Answer: She wanted to go to school.
Q7. Why did Chellappa refuse Ammu's wish?
Answer: He believed education was more useful for his son.
Q8. What work did Chellappa want Ammu to do?
Answer: Work as a maid.
Q9. Where was the big house?
Answer: On the hill.
Q10. Who was the new owner of the big house?
Answer: Mrs. Mohan.
Q11. What was Mrs. Mohan's marital status?
Answer: She was a widow.
Q12. How was Mrs. Mohan described?
Answer: She was slim and grey-haired.
Q13. What did Mrs. Mohan ask Ammu to do?
Answer: She asked Ammu to come and work at the house.
Q14. What attracted Ammu in Mrs. Mohan's house?
Answer: The bookcase.
Q15. What did Ammu love?
Answer: Books and learning.
Q16. What did Mrs. Mohan realize about Ammu?
Answer: She realized that Ammu was interested in studying.
Q17. What did Mrs. Mohan think of starting?
Answer: A small school for unfortunate girls.
Q18. What did Mrs. Mohan first offer to do for Ammu?
Answer: Pay her school fees.
Q19. Why did Ammu initially refuse the offer?
Answer: She feared her father would not agree.
Q20. What did Ammu worry about at home?
Answer: Who would work and care for her family.
Q21. What was Mrs. Mohan's second plan?
Answer: To teach Ammu herself.
Q22. What had Mrs. Mohan been before retirement?
Answer: A headmistress.
Q23. What did Mrs. Mohan begin teaching Ammu?
Answer: The alphabet.
Q24. Where did Mrs. Mohan teach Ammu?
Answer: At her house.
Q25. When did Ammu come for lessons?
Answer: After finishing her chores.
Q26. How did Ammu feel after the lesson?
Answer: She went home happily.
Q27. What was Ammu's first step towards her goal?
Answer: Learning the alphabet.
Q28. What major social issue does the story highlight?
Answer: Girls' access to education.
Q29. What did Chellappa think education would do for Chinni?
Answer: It would help him get a job.
Q30. What does the title 'The First Step' refer to?
Answer: Ammu beginning her education.
